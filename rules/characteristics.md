# Characteristics & Derived Statistics

The five Characteristics rate the body on a scale of roughly **1–20** (higher
possible). There is deliberately **no Intelligence stat** — Traits, Passions,
and Skills cover the mind.

## The five Characteristics

| Stat | Name | Measures |
|------|------|----------|
| **SIZ** | Size | Height/weight/mass; feeds Hit Points, Knockdown, Damage |
| **DEX** | Dexterity | Agility and coordination; feeds Movement |
| **STR** | Strength | Physical power; feeds Damage and Movement |
| **CON** | Constitution | Health and vitality; feeds Hit Points, Healing, Major Wound |
| **APP** | Appeal | Charm, presence, attractiveness; feeds some social Skills and number of Distinctive Features |

## Generating Characteristics

- **Constructed:** distribute **60 points**, none below 8 or above 15 (before cultural modifier).
- **Random:** roll per culture table (e.g. Cymric = `2D6+5` for each of SIZ/DEX/STR/CON/APP).
- **Cultural modifier:** e.g. Cymric characters get **+3 CON** after allocation.

An "average" Cymric knight has **SIZ 12, DEX 12, STR 12, CON 15, APP 13**
(used in the worked examples below).

## Derived statistics

| Derived stat | Formula | Cymric example |
|--------------|---------|----------------|
| Damage (dice) | (STR + SIZ) / 6 | (12+12)/6 = **4D6** |
| Brawling Damage | (STR + SIZ) / 6 points | 4 points |
| Healing Rate | CON / 5 | 15/5 = **3**/week |
| Total Hit Points | CON + SIZ | 15+12 = **27** |
| Unconscious | Total HP / 4 (round up) | 27/4 = **7** |
| Movement Rate | ((STR + DEX) / 2) + 5 | ((12+12)/2)+5 = **17** |
| Knockdown threshold | = SIZ | **12** |
| Major Wound threshold | = CON | **15** |

Round decimals of 0.5+ up.

## SIZ → height/weight (approx.)

| SIZ | Weight (lb) | Height |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| 8 | 95 | 4'11" |
| 10 | 125 | 5'5" |
| 12 | 144 | 5'8" |
| 14 | 165 | 5'11" |
| 16 | 189 | 6'3" |
| 18 | 218 | 6'6" |

(±5 lb / ±1½ in; muscular or heavy characters run shorter for their weight.)

## Quick NPC stat blocks

For commoners and minor NPCs you rarely need full stats. A serviceable default:

- **Commoner:** all Characteristics ~10–12; HP ~22; Damage 3–4D6.
- **Trained warrior / knight:** STR+SIZ ≥ 21 (Damage 4D6), CON 11+; a key
  weapon Skill around 12–18.

New-character guidance: a first character's **STR + SIZ should total ≥ 21**
(Damage 4D6); ≥ 27 gives Damage 5D6. CON should be at least 11 for decent HP.
