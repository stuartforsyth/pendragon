# Distinctive Features (Appearance)

**Distinctive Features** describe a character's most outstanding physical
qualities — the things a stranger would use to address them ("Hey, big-nose",
"Hey, pretty boy"). They are the go-to tool for describing an NPC at a glance.

## How many features (by APP)

The **APP** Characteristic determines how many features an NPC has, and whether
they skew positive or negative.

| APP | Appearance | Distinctive Features |
|-----|-----------|----------------------|
| 5 or less | Infirm | At death's door |
| 6–7 | Unseemly | Three negative |
| 8–9 | Ill-favored | Two negative |
| 10–12 | Plain | One negative, one positive |
| 13–15 | Fair | Two positive |
| 16–18 | Elegant | Three positive |
| 19+ | Surpassing | Four positive |

To generate: pick an APP (or roll it), read off the count, then draw that many
features from the tables below.

## Feature tables

### Physique
**Positive:** barrel-chested, big-shouldered, brawny, bull-necked, buxom,
coltish, curvy, full figure, hourglass figure, husky, muscular, petite, smells
like flowers, solid, strapping, statuesque, taut, willowy
**Negative:** a wisp of a thing, flabby, gangly, gawky, hunched, lanky,
overweight, paunchy, pigeon-chested, rotund, round-shouldered, skinny, smells
like feet, squat, stooped, sunken-chested

### Limbs
**Positive:** big biceps, delicate-fingered, graceful arms, manicured,
right-handed, strong hands and forearms
**Negative:** bow-legged, knock-kneed, left-handed, tiny or misshapen hands,
pigeon-toed, spindly, withered

### Hair
**Positive:** chestnut brown, curly, glossy black, flowing, golden blonde,
handsome grey, natural, shining silver, straight, thick, wavy
**Negative:** bald, mangy, patchy, prematurely grey or white, red hair of any
shade, thinning

### Face
**Positive:** big mustache, black eyes, blue eyes, bright-eyed, broad nose,
button nose, clean-shaven, dazzlingly white smile, gray eyes, luxurious combed
beard, Roman nose, smooth complexion, straight teeth
**Negative:** big ears, crooked or missing teeth, crumby mustache, droopy
eyelids, eyes of different colors, freckles, green eyes, malformed nose,
pockmarks, rough, scars, scraggly beard, unibrow

### Speech
**Positive:** charming accent, clear, deep, dulcet, lilting, resonant
**Negative:** bellowing, nasal, lisp, mumbly, scratchy, sharp, shrill, squeaky,
stutter, thick and unpleasant accent

## Period note (the "negative" list)

Medieval prejudice tagged **red hair, freckles, green eyes, and
left-handedness** as sinister — which is why they appear under "negative."
Downplay or ignore this in your own game as you prefer ("Your Pendragon Will
Vary").

## Example NPC features (from published characters)

Two short phrases make a memorable NPC:
Strawberry-blond locks; mischievous · Searching eyes; broad-shouldered ·
Bushy eyebrows; charming accent · Kindly eyes; pot-bellied · Scraggly beard;
hooded eyes · Steel-grey hair; piercing gaze · Wispy mustache; intense gaze ·
Bulldog countenance; tow-headed · Powerful build; magnificent bearing.

## Using appearance for an NPC

1. Choose or roll **APP** → look up the feature count.
2. Draw that many features (positive/negative per the table) from **different**
   categories (e.g. one Face + one Physique).
3. State them as a short phrase — that's the NPC's visual signature.
