# Passions

Passions are intense beliefs that can **inspire** a character (boosting a single
Skill for a short time) or **compel** them to act. A Passion value **over 15**
compels the character to act upon it. Passions grant temporary modifiers to
Skills and Traits, but never to Characteristics.

## Passion levels

| Level | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Unsung | 1–4 |
| Normal | 5–15 |
| Famous | 16–20 (triggered easily; narrows autonomy) |
| Exalted | 20 (+x) (must always act on it; rare) |

## The four courts

All Passions except **Honor** belong to one of four "courts." **The sum of
Passion values within a court must never exceed 40.**

### Fidelitas (faithfulness / loyalty)
- **Homage (Lord)** — sworn loyalty to one's liege (household knights start with this)
- **Fealty** — an alternative sworn loyalty
- **Loyalty (Group)** — e.g. Loyalty (Vassals), Loyalty (a rescuer)

### Fervor (love & hate)
- **Love (Family)**
- **Love (Person or Group)**
- **Hate (Person or Group)** — e.g. Hate (Saxons)

### Adoratio (worship / adoration)
- **Devotion (Deity)** — religious devotion
- **Adoration (Beloved)** — courtly/romantic adoration

### Civilitas (social ideals)
- **Hospitality**
- **Station** — pride in and duty to one's social rank
- **Chivalry**
- **Romance** (an Ideal built from several Passions)

**Honor** stands apart, transcending the courts. Knights need **Honor 5+** to
qualify for their station and retain knighthood.

## Universal Passions

All peoples of Britain share these, learned from childhood and respected even
between enemies: **Homage/Fealty**, **Love (Family)**, **Hospitality**, and
**Station**.

## Cultural Passions

Homelands add inherited Passions. For example, folk of **Salisbury** during the
Boy King period share **Hate (Saxons)**.

## Typical starting values (constructed method)

| Passion | Start |
|---------|-------|
| Honor | 15 |
| Homage (Lord) | 15 |
| Love (Family) | 10 |
| Station | 10 |
| Hospitality | 10 |
| Devotion (Deity) | 5 |
| Hate (Saxons) — Salisbury only | 5 |

Then distribute an additional **15 points** among these and any others.

A **newly gained** Passion (from a dramatic event) starts at `1D6+6`.

## Using Passions for an NPC

Give an NPC **one motivating Passion** to make them adventure-relevant, e.g.
`Hate (Saxons) 16`, `Love (Family) 14`, `Loyalty (Lord) 15`, or a Devotion tied
to their religion. A Famous Passion (16+) is a ready-made plot hook.
