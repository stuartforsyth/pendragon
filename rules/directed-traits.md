# Directed Traits & Obsessions

**Directed Traits** are feelings focused on a specific individual or group that
override a character's general attitude. They are written as a modifier against
a base Trait, e.g. `*Suspicious (King Mark) +5`. When a roll involving that
Trait concerns the named object, apply the modifier.

These make excellent NPC hooks: a grudge, a fear, a loyalty aimed at a named
person or faction.

## Obsessions (extreme Directed Traits)

An Obsession is an intense Directed Trait that demands attention whenever its
object comes up. Redeemed slowly in the Winter Phase, or via an opposed
**Honor vs. Obsession** resolution.

| Obsession | Extreme form of | Start value | In brief |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|----------|
| **Avarice** | Selfish | +2D6 to Selfish | Must possess a coveted object; lies, cheats, steals to get it |
| **Fear** | Cowardly | +1D6+5 to Cowardly | Flees or refuses to engage the feared thing |
| **Jealousy** | Suspicious | +2D6 to Suspicious | Must harm a "Rival" who threatens a relationship |

Other Directed Traits commonly seen: **Love**, **Hate**, **Loyalty**,
**Mistrust**, directed at a named person or group.

### Effect examples
- **Avarice:** not having the object causes Misery; must attempt to acquire it whenever possible.
- **Fear:** on confronting the object, roll Fear — success = retreat, critical = flee in terror.
- **Jealousy:** must try to harm the Rival (socially or physically); in the Rival's company, `−5` to Forgiving, Just, Merciful, Prudent, Spiritual, Trusting.

## Using Directed Traits for an NPC

Pick a base trait, a named target, and a `+3` to `+10` modifier:

- `*Vengeful (the man who killed my brother) +10`
- `*Suspicious (all Saxons) +5`
- `*Loyal (Countess of Salisbury) +5`

One line like this instantly gives an NPC a personal stake in the story.
