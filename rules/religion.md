# Religion

Religion shapes an NPC's favoured Personality Traits, their Devotion Passion,
and their outlook. A "Religious" NPC (a devout exemplar) has all six favoured
traits high; ordinary folk simply lean toward them.

## Religions of Arthurian Britain

### British Christian
The most common faith in Logres. Some are influenced by the **Pelagian Heresy**
(a British strand more optimistic about human nature than Roman orthodoxy).

- **Favoured Traits:** Chaste, Forgiving, Merciful, Modest, Spiritual, Temperate
- **Religious Knight benefit:** +4 Hit Points
- **Requirements (to be a Religious knight):** 96 Virtue Points (min 16 each), Devotion (God) 16+, Religion (Christian) 10+
- **Demeanour:** often serious, otherworldly; strives to balance martial life with the Gospels.

### Roman Christian
The orthodox, centralised Roman church (Council of Nicaea, Original Sin,
salvation through the sacraments). Same virtues as British Christian but more
hierarchical and orthodox; associated with Roman-descended and Continental folk.

- **Favoured Traits:** Chaste, Forgiving, Merciful, Modest, Spiritual, Temperate
- **Benefit / requirements:** as British Christian.

### Pagan (British Paganism)
Polytheistic; the divine is in all things. The "opposite" of their Christian
kin. Common among Cymri, Irish, and Picts.

- **Favoured Traits:** Lustful, Energetic, Generous, Honest, Proud, Spiritual
- **Religious Knight benefit:** +2 Healing Rate
- **Requirements:** 96 Virtue Points (min 16 each), Devotion (Gods) 16+, Religion (Pagan) 10+
- **Sample deities:** Beli-Lugh (sun, music, war, healing), Brigid/Brigantia (fire, poetry, war), Bran (life/death/underworld), Cerridwen (magic), Diana (the hunt), Blodeuwedd, Creiddylad, Camulos & Cocidius (war).

### Wodinic / Wotanic (Saxon Heathenry)
The Saxon warrior faith. Lives for battle; fatalistic about the Fates.

- **Favoured Traits:** Generous, Indulgent, Proud, Reckless, Valorous, Worldly
- **Religious Knight benefit:** +1D6 Damage
- **Requirements:** 96 Virtue Points (min 16 each), Devotion (Wodin) 16+, Religion (Wodinic) 10+
- **Sample deities (the Ese):** Wodin, Baeldaeg (light/sun), Eostre (spring), Frigg (fertility), Haleg (harvest), Heil (healing).

## Suggested culture → religion defaults

Use these as sensible defaults for an NPC (individuals vary):

| Culture | Likely religion |
|---------|-----------------|
| Cymri | British Christian or Pagan |
| Roman | Roman Christian |
| Aquitanian | Roman Christian |
| Frankish | Roman Christian (some pagan) |
| Irish | Pagan or British Christian |
| Pict | Pagan |
| Saxon | Wodinic (Heathen) |

## Using religion for an NPC

Pick the religion (default by culture), then bias the NPC's key Traits toward
its favoured list and give them a matching **Devotion** Passion (e.g.
`Devotion (God)`, `Devotion (Gods)`, `Devotion (Wodin)`).
