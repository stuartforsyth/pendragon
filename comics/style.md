Comic-book art in the style of Hal Foster's Prince Valiant: lush, painterly ink and watercolour, confident brush linework, dramatic but readable staging, classic Arthurian adventure. Muted earthy palette: mud brown, iron grey, wool-dyed red, ochre, moss green, overcast skies. Heroic and a little grim; blood is implied, never graphic. No text, letters, speech balloons or sound effects anywhere in the image. Leave a clear parchment-coloured caption band along the bottom of each panel, empty.

Setting and period accuracy: Britain in the Boy King period of King Arthur's reign (early 6th century, Pendragon 6th edition). Timber halls, hillforts, Roman ruins and open ridge country; no stone castles with turrets. Knights wear a padded aketon under a mail hauberk with mail coif, a nasal helm, and carry a large kite-shaped shield and spear or sword. No plate armour, no visored helms, no tournament pavilions, no stirrup-free cavalry errors (riders sit on saddles with stirrups). Cloaks pinned with brooches, long wool tunics, leather belts. Painted shield devices and plain pennons are allowed. Foes: Picts are unarmoured, painted men with small bucklers and long spears; Irish kerns are unarmoured with simple shields and spears; Cambrian archers wear plain wool and carry longbows; Lothian knights are armoured like the heroes but in their own colours. Noble wear for non-battle scenes: long dyed wool tunics with embroidered hems, mantles, gold brooches and torcs.
