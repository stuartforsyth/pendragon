Character sheet (keep these looks identical in every panel; edit here, then reuse in every session).

Noble wear (non-battle scenes): each wears their colour (Erik black, Cwyllog blue, Emrys green) as a long wool tunic with an embroidered hem, a mantle pinned with a brooch, and a plain belt.
