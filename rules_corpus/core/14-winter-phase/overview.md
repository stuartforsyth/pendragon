The annual **Winter Phase** has 11 steps: **1** Solo Scenario · **2** Personal Events ·
**3** Experience Rolls · **4** Economic Circumstances · **5** Aging & Old Age · **6**
Squire & Maiden-in-Waiting · **7** Horse Survival · **8** Training & Practice · **9**
Tally Glory · **10** Prestige Reward · **11** Family.

## Step 1 — Solo Scenario

Run any applicable Solo first (especially for a missed game year) to acquire checks. See
[[core.solo-scenarios.standard-activity-solos]].

## Step 2 — Personal Events (Table 14.1)

Roll **1D20 on Table 14.1: Personal Events** for a tested Trait/Passion, then make an
unopposed roll against it:

- **Critical:** make an immediate experience roll (Step 3) for it **and** check it.
- **Success:** check it.
- **Failure:** (Trait) roll the opposite and check if successful.
- **Fumble:** (Trait) check the opposite; (Passion) lose 1 point.

(Roll 20 = player's choice of Trait/Passion.)

## Step 3 — Experience Rolls

For **each experience check** on the sheet, **roll 1D20**: if the roll is **greater than
the current value**, the Statistic **increases by 1**. A value at **20 (with or without a
critical bonus) requires a 20** to raise. **One roll per checked Statistic per year**;
all checked Statistics are rolled. (The higher the value, the less likely it grows.)
