## Step 4 — Economic Circumstances

Set by last year's **Standard of Living**.

- **Impoverished:** lose squire, horses, and **2 Honor**; −1 to child-survival (worsening
  yearly); immediate **−1 SIZ/STR/APP** (cumulative, restored after a year Ordinary+); all
  armor **−1 permanently per year**; a CON roll or lose 1 CON; clothing halved; childbirth
  **−3**.
- **Poor:** horses & children **−1** survival; each horse loses 10 Move, 3 CON, 1D6 Charge
  damage; immediate −1 SIZ/STR/APP (+ CON roll); armor **−1**; clothing halved.
- **Income:** a vassal knight nets ~**£1 discretionary** from £10 lands (£6 maintenance,
  £3 soldiers/servants). Household knights get maintenance from their lord (often +£1 at
  Yule).
- **Maintenance (Table 14.2):** household knights are kept at **Ordinary** (£4 bachelor /
  £6 married); mercenary/vassal knights **choose** how much to pay for next year's Standard
  of Living. **Raising emergency coin:** call in a Favor (F1 = £1) — a vassal asking his
  liege owes **F1 per £1**.

## Step 5 — Aging & Old Age

Age +1 each Winter Phase. From **age 35**, check **Senescence** and **Infirmity** yearly.

**Senescence:** roll **2D6 on Table 14.3: Aging** for the number of rolls on **Table 14.4:
Characteristic Lost**.

| 2D6 | Characteristics affected |
|-----|--------------------------|
| 2 / 12 | 4 |
| 3 / 11 | 3 |
| 4 / 10 | 2 |
| 5 / 9 | 1 |
| 6–8 | 0 |

**Table 14.4 — Characteristic Lost (1D6):** 1 SIZ (stooping) · 2 DEX (palsy) · 3 STR
(weakness) · 4 CON (illness) · 5 APP (deformation) · 6 none. Recompute derived stats on
each loss. (Also used for Major/Mortal wounds, magic, madness, etc.)

- **Infirmity:** when any Characteristic **other than CON reaches 5**. The character
  **retires** (turns title/lands/arms to the heir, keeps remaining Passive Glory). Aging
  losses at 5 or below are taken from **CON** instead (the rolled stat stays at 5).
- **Decrepitude:** when **CON reaches 5** — bedridden, out of play, no more Glory; still
  ages. Each year roll **1D6 vs CON**: higher than CON = death.
- **Death:** a failed Decrepitude Roll, or **CON 0**.
