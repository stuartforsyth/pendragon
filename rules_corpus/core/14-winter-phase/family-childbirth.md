Step 11 resolves the year's **births and deaths** (the event may have happened any time;
it's determined now). A Prestige Reward may guarantee a healthy birth or a child's gender
(own children only).

## Conception & childbirth

When conception is possible, the woman rolls **CON** (assume CON 13 if unspecified) with
modifiers: **−10** if she had a child last year; **−X** per year over 35; **−1** Poor; **−1
per year** Impoverished. If modified CON ≤ 0 or the marriage is barren, don't roll.

- **Critical:** roll 1D6 — 1–4 **Multiple Birth** (Table 14.11); 5 Blessed Birth boy; 6
  Blessed Birth girl (Table 14.12).
- **Success:** healthy child (boy on an odd roll, girl on even).
- **Failure:** no conception.
- **Fumble:** if a child was born last year, none this year; otherwise **Tragedy** (Table
  14.8).

### Table 14.8 — Tragedy (1D6, +1 if first conception)

| 1–2 | Mother and child die |
| 3–4 | Mother dies, healthy child born |
| 5 | Mother now barren, healthy child born |
| 6 | Mother now barren, child dies |
| 7 | No child, barren marriage |

**PC childbirth (better odds):** on a Tragedy, the mother's player may fight for life —
**CON** roll (live, −1 CON; else −2 and try **STR**), then **STR** (live, −1 STR; else −2
and try **SIZ**), then **SIZ** (live, −1 SIZ; else dies). **Midwifery:** a Wisewoman's
successful Midwife roll may alter one death outcome (not a barren result).

Sons inherit the **father's** Family Characteristic; daughters the **mother's**.

## Survival & special births

- **Table 14.9 — Child Survival:** age 1 dies on **1–4**; ages 2–5 die on **1** (else
  healthy). Impoverished **−2**, Poor **−1**. **Table 14.10 — Cause of Death (1D6):** 1–3
  illness, 4–5 accident, 6 missing/presumed dead.
- **Table 14.11 — Multiple Birth:** 1–7 boy+girl twins; 8–10 fraternal girls; 11–13
  fraternal boys; 14–16 identical girls; 17–19 identical boys; 20 triplets.
- **Table 14.12 — Blessed Birth:** never rolls for survival (guaranteed to adulthood) and
  grants a bonus — e.g. +1 CON/APP/DEX/STR, or +5 to a Skill (Awareness, Compose,
  Horsemanship, Orate, etc.).
