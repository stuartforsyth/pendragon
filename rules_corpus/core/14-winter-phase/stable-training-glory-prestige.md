## Step 6 — Squire & Maiden-in-Waiting

Everyone ages +1. Squires are released at **21** (become esquires). An attached esquire
(£1/yr) may leave: roll **1D20 vs (years served past 20 + 5)** — success = departs.
**Maidens (Table 14.5):** roll 1D20 (**20+ = marries and departs**); +2 per year of
service 17–20, +5 per year past 20. Other Supporting Characters check **Table 14.6:
Departure** (1 death, 2 depart, 3 depart, 4–20 stay).

## Step 7 — Horse Survival (Table 14.7)

Roll **1D20 for each charger, destrier, or unique horse** (household knights' ordinary
sumpters/rouncys are replaced automatically to a minimum stable of 1 charger, 2 rouncys,
1 sumpter):

| 1D20 | Result |
|------|--------|
| ≤1 | Horse dies/unusable (not even sellable) |
| 2 | Roll Horsemanship: crit = healthy; success = **Weakened** (−2 Move, −1D6 HP, −1 to future survival rolls); failure = double those + −1D6 Charge damage; fumble = dies |
| 3–20 | Healthy |

## Step 8 — Training & Practice (pick one)

- **Trait:** +1 (opposite −1; min 1); **no Trait above 19**; no critical bonus.
- **Passion:** +1 or −1; **max 20**; no critical bonus; court 40-cap displacement applies.
- **Characteristic:** +1 to STR/DEX/CON/APP (**not after age 35**; SIZ only via Getting
  Fat).
- **Skills up to 15:** distribute **6 points** among Skills under 15 (none over 15).
- **A Skill up to 20:** raise one Skill over 15 by **+1** (max 20; no critical bonus).

**Getting Fat:** SIZ may be raised 3 times after 21, each adding a feature — 1st
Heavyset; 2nd Portly (−1 DEX); 3rd Obese (−1 DEX, −1 CON). Obese characters pay more for
fitting, may be too heavy for small horses, and gain **20 Passive Glory/year**.

## Step 9 — Tally Glory

Sum all Glory from the year (including Winter Phase) into Total Glory. **Passive Glory**
accrues from annual benchmarks; adventures/quests/challenges are the primary source.

## Step 10 — Prestige Reward

**One per full 1,000 Glory**, spendable only in the Winter Phase (may be saved):

- Raise **any Statistic +1** (no restrictions; may add/raise a Skill critical bonus over
  20).
- Lower a Passion by 1.
- Guarantee an **heir** conceived, healthy, and of chosen gender — or the conception/
  health/gender of any child.

If a Trait/Passion reaches 16 (or an Ideal qualifies) **via** a Prestige Reward, the
corresponding Passive Glory waits until next year, but the **bonuses apply immediately**.
