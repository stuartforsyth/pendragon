## Donning armor

Fully arming up (a leisure action, with a squire) takes **5 minutes = 30 Combat
Rounds**. Time is **tripled** with no squire, **halved** with two helpers.

In an **emergency**: down to **15 rounds** with a successful **DEX roll and Squire Skill
roll**; failing one → **20 rounds**; failing both → full time. The knight may take no
other action but communication while donning. **Padding alone** (no squire) takes rounds
equal to its Armor Protection value; a **helmet** takes **1 round**.

## Stacking

**All Armor Protection values stack** — total every worn piece for the character's full
Armor Protection. You may go into combat **partially armored**.

## Penalties

Wearing armor **never** affects the DEX Balance Roll to stay on foot or mounted.

- **Hot weather:** Combat/Weapon Skills take **−5**, worsening **−1 per consecutive day**
  of excessive heat to a **max −10**.
- **Sleeping in armor:** sleeping in mail or plate temporarily reduces **all Skills −1
  per night**; one good night's sleep out of armor removes it.
