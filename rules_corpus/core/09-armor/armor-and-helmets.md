Armor is **Mail**, **Plate**, or **Textile** (padding). Textile padding (aketon) is worn
under mail.

## Table 9.1 — Suits of Armor (total Armor Protection)

| Typical for | Components | Protection | Shield |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Squire | Gambeson, open helm | 5 | Round (Large) |
| Poor Knights | Mail hauberk, open helm, aketon | 9 | Kite (Large) |
| **Knights** | Mail hauberk, nasal helm, aketon | 10 | Kite (Large) |
| Armored guardsmen & squires | Haubergeon, open helm, aketon | 7 | Kite (Large) |
| Rich knights | Advanced hauberk, advanced nasal helm, aketon | 12 | Kite (Large) |
| Very rich knights (515+) | Coat of plates, advanced hauberk, advanced nasal helm, aketon | 13 | Kite (Large); −5 Move on foot |
| Foot soldiers | Gambeson, kettle helm | 5 | Kite (Large) |
| Saxon footmen | Haubergeon, open helm | 5 | Round (Large) |
| Irish footmen | None | 0 | Simple (Med.) |
| Picts | None | 0 | Buckler (Small) |
| Roman foot soldiers | Haubergeon, advanced nasal helm, aketon | 9 | Scutum (Large) |

A large shield penalizes **−2 to all Weapon Skills when mounted** (except a Charge with
spear or lance).

## Table 9.2 — Armor

|------|-----------|------|-----------|---------|
| Aketon | All | Textile | 2 | None |

| Haubergeon | All | Mail | 4 | Aketon |

| Coat of Plates | Late Boy King (515+) | Plate | 6 | Aketon |

**Coat of plates** worn over a (advanced) hauberk gives **+1 Armor Protection** and
**negates weapon damage bonuses vs Mail** (bonuses vs **Plate** still apply), but imposes
**−5 Movement Rate on foot**.

## Table 9.3 — Helmets

| Item | Available | Protection |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Kettle Helm | All | 1 |
| Open Helm | All | 1 |

| Advanced Nasal Helm | Boy King | 3 |

Most early helms include a **coif** (mail hood) whose protection is already factored in.

### Closed helms

Closed helms (starting with the Advanced Nasal Helm) impose **−5 to all sight/sound
Awareness rolls**. Raising a **visor** nullifies the penalty but **halves the helm's
Armor Protection**; a visorless closed helm must be **removed** (1 full Combat Round) to
nullify it. Assume visors are down unless stated.
