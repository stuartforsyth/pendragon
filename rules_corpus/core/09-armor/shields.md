Shields need **no separate Skill** and are used with one-handed weapons. A shield gives
its Armor Value when the wielder scores a **partial success** (a failure lets the hit
**bypass** it). A **mounted** character may apply Shield Protection to their **horse** on
a partial success. An aware shield-user imposes a **Missile Attack Penalty** on archers.

## Shield sizes

| Size | Armor Protection | Missile penalty | Notes |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Small** | 4 (melee only, not vs Mounted Charge) | none | useless vs missiles |
| **Medium** | 6 | −5 | compromise |
| **Large** | 6 | −10 | **−2 Weapon Skill mounted** (except as noted) |

## Table 9.4 — Individual shields

- **Buckler (Small)** — center-grip round/oval; **+1D6 damage** to a Barehand punch while
  held; may be **dropped free** at a round's start.
- **Targe (Small)** — strapped to the forearm (hand free for javelins / a second weapon /
  a two-handed grip — but then gives **no protection**); **cannot be dropped**; doesn't
  hamper Grappling/Brawling; no punch bonus.
- **Simple (Medium)** — poor infantry shield; **shatters** if it blocks a **critical
  success** (useless afterward).
- **Round (Large)** — infantry/Saxon shield, central grip behind a boss.
- **Kite (Large)** — teardrop; the standard knightly shield of the Boy King Period.
  **Ignores the −2 mounted penalty** when used with a couched spear/lance in a Mounted
  Charge; designed to also protect against arrows.
- **Scutum (Large)** — oval Roman shield with brackets holding weighted javelins
  (*plumbatae*).
