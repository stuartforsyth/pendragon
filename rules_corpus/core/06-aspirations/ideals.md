Ideals grant **bonuses** for maintaining minimum values in prescribed Skills, Passions,
and Traits. Most are realized through action (not bestowal); benefits accrue as soon as
qualified and **lapse if a requirement drops below minimum**. Some Ideals are mutually
exclusive; others may be held together.

## Chivalrous Knight

**Benefits:** may invoke the **Chivalry** Passion when offended vs the Code; **Armor of
Virtue** = 4 points of Armor Protection that blocks *all* damage (even damage that
bypasses armor — falls, fire); **150 Passive Glory/year**; **Protection from Magic &
Miracles** — a Chivalry roll (success/critical) grants immunity to any supernatural
effect that would force a breach of the Code.

**Requirements:**
- Chivalrous **Traits** total **96** among: Energetic, Generous, Just, Merciful,
  Modest, Valorous (no per-Trait minimum — an average high quality).
- **Chivalry 15, Station 10, Hospitality 10.** Deliberately violating the Code may
  cost the Ideal.

## Religious Knight

**Benefits (by faith):** Christian **+4 Total HP**; Pagan **+2 Damage, +2 Healing
Rate**; Wodinic **+1D6 Damage**; Heathen **+2 Movement, +1 Healing Rate**. Plus: may
invoke **Devotion (Deity)** in defense of the faith; **100 Passive Glory/year**;
**Protection from Magic & Miracles** — a Devotion roll (success/critical) grants
immunity to magic (if Christian) or miracles (if Pagan/Wodinic/Heathen).

**Requirements:** value **16 in all six** virtuous Traits of the faith —
- Christian: Chaste, Forgiving, Merciful, Modest, Spiritual, Temperate
- Pagan: Energetic, Generous, Honest, Lustful, Proud, Spiritual
- Wodinic: Generous, Indulgent, Proud, Reckless, Valorous, Worldly
- Heathen: Arbitrary, Honest, Proud, Spiritual, Suspicious, Vengeful

  — plus **Devotion (Deity) 16** and **Religion 10**.

## Romantic Knight

The champion of Love (above duty/Honor); a practitioner of *fine amor* (a **Lover** with
an Adoration for their **Beloved**).

**Benefits:** always finds favor at Queen Guenever's court (may join the Queen's Knights
on request); **+2 base Geniality** always; **double Glory at tournaments** when wearing
the Beloved's favor; **Protection from Magic & Miracles** — an Adoration roll
(success/critical) grants immunity to any effect that would make them harm/betray the
Beloved.

**Requirements:** Romantic **Traits** total **90** among Chaste, Generous, Honest,
Modest, Spiritual (as a Directed Trait toward the Beloved), Temperate; plus **Adoration
(Beloved) 10**.
