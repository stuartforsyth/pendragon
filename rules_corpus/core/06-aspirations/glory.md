Glory is fame accumulated for deeds. **Only nobles gain it; it can increase but never
decrease** — successful *bad* behavior adds Glory as surely as good (bad behavior costs
Honor, not Glory). Glory requires **public acclamation** (secret deeds earn none) and
decides **precedence** when other factors are equal, but never overrides rank (a count
with 3,000 outranks a baron with 5,000). **Every 1,000 Glory → one Prestige Reward**
(Winter Phase, Step 10).

## Gaining Glory — benchmarks

Any non-routine act with notable, **public** consequences may win Glory. **Never award
more than 1,000 for a single event**, though separate awards can combine past 1,000.

- **25** (slightly risky): defeat bandits; defeat a foe with Skill 15.
- **50** (dangerous): defeat a foe with Skill 20.
- **100** (heroic): kill a panther; save your liege's life.
- **250** (extraordinary): kill a dangerous monster (large giant, gryphon); win the
  Pentecost Tournament; become a vassal knight.
- **300–500** (mighty): kill a fearsome monster (gargantuan giant, wyrm); become a
  banneret.
- **1,000** (once-in-a-lifetime): become a knight; become a great lord; join the Round
  Table; attend the Mass of the Holy Grail.
- **None:** routine with no risk/consequence.

## "Do You Know Who I Am?" — challenges

A knight refusing a fight normally loses 1 Honor for cowardice, **but** a knight of
greater rank or **≥5,000 Glory more** may decline an inferior's challenge without risk
(backing down from a **social better** earns a **Prudent** check; declining a
challenge "to the death" also earns a **Cowardly** check).

To fight a social better, the inferior must master fear: roll **Valorous** at **−1 per
1,000 Glory difference (max −5)**; they may first roll **Prudent** at **+1 per 1,000
(max +5)** to back down honorably. A disguised knight (hidden/changed arms) imposes
**−10 to Recognize**. A Round Table Knight may ride through an enemy formation,
intimidating all so none dares approach.
