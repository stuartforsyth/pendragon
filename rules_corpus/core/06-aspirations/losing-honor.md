It is far easier to lose Honor than gain it. When a violation occurs, the GM either
docks Honor immediately or has the PK roll the **opposing** Trait/Passion — on a
**success** they lose the Honor and check the opposing Trait.

## Honor loss by acting against Traits/Passions

- **−1:** seduce a married noble / cuckold (Chaste); idle in a crisis (Energetic);
  lie/cheat/steal (Honest); skip a feast in your honor or refuse a toast (Indulgent);
  virility questioned (Lustful); over-boast (Modest); first scolding by Sir Kay
  (Proud); hold back (Reckless); dress as a commoner / insult or provoke a noble
  (Station); disobey liege on a small matter (Homage/Fealty); insult a guest/host
  (Hospitality).
- **−2:** be cuckolded (Chaste); defraud/slander (Just); attack an unarmed knight or
  kill one who begged mercy / was ransomable (Merciful); chronic glutton/drunk or vomit
  at a feast (Temperate); use missiles vs a knight or set lethal traps (Valorous);
  refuse/argue an order (Fealty/Homage); defer to a wealthy commoner / embarrass a
  noble (Station); physically harm a guest (Hospitality).
- **−3:** perjury (Just); deliberately harm a lady (Merciful); flee a fair fight /
  surrender to an equal (Valorous); refuse aid to family (Love Family); disobey liege on
  a significant matter (Fealty/Homage); harm your host (Hospitality); do commoner's work
  (Station).
- **−4:** kidnap a lady (Merciful); survive your liege's death as his bodyguard
  (Valorous); defer to a poor commoner (Station).
- **−5:** break an oath (Honest); kill a lady or holy person of your faith (Spiritual);
  kill by poison (Valorous); cause a battle loss in a liege's service (Fealty/Homage);
  kill a guest/host (Hospitality).
- **−10:** unjustly kill a family member (Love Family); **betray your liege** — reveal
  secrets, harm, kill, oppose in battle (Fealty/Homage).

Also: **inaction** (ignoring others' dishonor, or failing to avenge an insult/slain
family) = −1; **losing** a formal honor-challenge = −1; **killing a surrendered
knight** = −5 (**−10 if Chivalrous**).

## Accusation (formal stages)

An accusation by a **reliable source** (equal/greater status, Honorable, credible
witness), made before peers, proceeds in stages — each an accuser's roll opposed by the
accused's Honor, up to a year apart. The accuser stakes **1–10 Honor**.

1. **Gossip** (Intrigue) → 2. **Public Accusation** (Orate) → 3. **Testimony**, sworn
on relics/sword (Just) → 4. **Guilt** proven/disproven (special — often trial of arms,
argument before the liege, or word-vs-word Honor roll). Proven → accused loses the
stake; disproven → **accuser** loses it.

**Overlooking petty issues:** a knight may ignore an inferior's accusation (a known
liar, or someone with < half their Glory) with a successful unopposed **Honor** roll.

## Grievous Dishonor (instant −10, often plus punishment)

Breaking & entering; Fratricide/Sororicide; Murder (perfidious killing outside honorable
combat); Parricide (→ outlawry; intentional → loss of all Honor & knighthood); Rape
(capital); Rebellion/Treason (capital); **Working Magic** (→ loss of knightly status).

## False claims (regaining Honor via apology)

- **Gossip** → public apology (both check Forgiving & Courtesy on acceptance).
- **Public accusation** → public retraction on the knees (Courtesy/Modest checks); if
  not forgiven, the accuser loses Honor equal to the victim's loss (victim checks
  Vengeful; if forgiven, Forgiving).
- **False testimony** → public humiliation, blows, reparations, a cleric's scourging;
  if mercy denied, the miscreant takes the same Honor loss they caused.

## Honorable Flight

A knight **must** risk death to save the king, their liege, or family. Acting for
themselves, they may flee — no shame from a dragon, a real giant, or 5:1 odds
(fleeing merely-outnumbering bandits at 5:1 is cowardly, −1 Honor). Prudent withdrawal
is fine at **half Total HP**, **2:1 odds or worse** by equal forces, as a ruse, or to
diminish danger.
