The Round Table is unveiled at Arthur's wedding in **514**; a **siege** (seat) is the
most prestigious honor in the land, offered **only by King Arthur himself**. Four kinds
hold sieges: **Chivalrous** paragons; ordinary knights who accomplish great deeds
(most PKs); Arthur's relations and allied kings' children; and redemption-project
"guests of the crown" (wards/hostages).

## Benefits

- **+5 Honor** on obtaining the siege.
- **1,000 Glory** on taking the oath.
- Annual gift of **£10**.
- Membership as a **Household Knight of King Arthur** (keeping prior honors): mutual
  protection (no member may harm another), a private house in Camelot with seven
  servants, upkeep as a **Rich knight**, the best steeds and armor.
- A siege with the knight's name written **magically in gold**.

## Requirements

There must be a **vacancy** (a member dies or is banished — rare after the Peace of
Arthur until the Grail Quest; GM's call). The knight must be **noticed and invited** by
Arthur — most likely for **Renowned (8,000+ Glory)**, having achieved the **Chivalry**
Ideal, or an extraordinary deed. Suggested minimum Passions: **Honor 15, Homage (King
Arthur) 16, Devotion (Deity) 10, Station 10, Hospitality 10, Chivalry 15.**

## Duties & expulsion

A member is expected to be a paragon of Arthur's ideals, do the king's bidding, and
attend Camelot every **Pentecost**; otherwise their life continues as before (same
lands, same associates, adventuring with the other PKs).

**Expulsion** follows a witnessed dishonorable crime brought to Arthur and a trial by
peers: the golden name vanishes, gifts are reclaimed, family cast out, retainer
canceled, exile from Camelot, and **−10 Honor** (plus the trial's loss). If Honor hits
0, they are stripped of knighthood in a public degradation (shield smashed, sword
broken, spurs cut off) and named enemy of the Round Table — any good knight may take
their life. They are recommended to an abbey to expiate their sins under the church's
protection.
