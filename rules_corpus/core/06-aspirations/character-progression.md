Characters change over time: Skills improve, Traits/Passions rise and fall,
Characteristics diminish with age. Advancement is variable; most never climb the whole
ladder, and gaining a **noble title** is exceedingly rare. Parenthesized steps are
optional.

- **Knights:** Squire → (Esquire) → Mercenary Knight *or* Household Knight → (Officer)
  → Vassal Knight → (Courtier) → Banneret Knight → (Noble Title)
- **Ladies:** Handmaiden → Lady → (Noble Title) → Gentlewoman

## "Winning the game"

A player may set a **lifelong ambition** and, on reaching it, **retire** the character
to personal fulfillment — they've "won." Such a character may still be played when
action occurs near their retirement, when their particular Skill is needed, or when
everyone is mustered for a battle.
