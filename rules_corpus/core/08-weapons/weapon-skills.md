Each melee Weapon Skill covers a **suite** of related weapons — you fight equally well
with any weapon under the same Skill (switch a battle axe for a mace mid-fight, both
**Hafted**; but a sword uses **Sword**). Every knight must master **Charge, Sword, and
Brawling**. **All Weapon Skills default to DEX/2** at creation (from DEX at 14).

## The Skills

- **Charge** — used *instead of* the weapon's own Skill when mounted and moving fast
  enough for a **Mounted Charge** (p.140); its value supersedes the wielded weapon's.
  Never for bare hands / Brawling weapons. Charging with a **Long** weapon vs a foe
  without one gives **+5**. **Lances** are the only weapons used exclusively with Charge.
- **Sword** — any long-bladed weapon, one or two hands. No notable strengths/weaknesses;
  the most generally effective type and the symbol of knighthood.
- **Spear** — long-hafted stabbing weapons in close combat, one or two hands. Versatile
  but fragile: **all spears break on a fumble or on a tie vs a sword-wielder**. Throwable
  ones also use Thrown Weapon.
- **Hafted / Two-Handed Hafted** — axes, maces, hammers (one/two hands). Advantages vary
  by type; break on a fumble or on a tie vs a sword.
- **Flail** — the war flail (post-Boy King) needs its own Skill; two-handed (**no
  shield**); breaks on fumble/tie-vs-sword.
- **Brawling** — fists, wrestling, knives/daggers at Close Quarters/Contact, all Brawling
  actions and improvised weapons.
- **Bow** — wood/horn missile weapon; knights use it for hunting, not usually combat.
- **Crossbow** — any crossbow; needs far less training than a bow.
- **Thrown Weapon** — any thrown implement (spears, javelins, daggers, axes, stones).

## Parry Protection

Swords and some two-handed weapons give **Parry +x** (x = 2–4) when used without a
shield — works like Shield Protection: a **Partial Success** triggers the bonus to Armor
Protection. **Shield and Parry Protection cannot both apply to the same attack.**

A **secondary off-hand weapon** (instead of a shield) grants **+2 Parry Protection**
(or **+1** if the main hand holds a sword), on top of the main weapon's Parry; it
**cannot be used offensively**.

## One-handed weapons, shields & handedness

Knights usually pair a one-handed weapon with a shield. Without a shield, a one-handed
weapon may be gripped in two hands but **does not gain the +2D6** of a true two-handed
weapon (GM may rule such a two-handed grip avoids dropping the weapon on a fumble). All
combatants are trained **right-handed** (a lone left-hander ruins close formation).
