Bows deal the character's own Weapon Damage **−1D6**, but each is **capped** regardless
of the wielder (a 5D6 character with a longbow deals 4D6; only 3D6 with a self bow).
Ranges are detailed under Missile Ranges (p.152; see
[[core.combat.missile-weapons]]).

## Table 8.2 — Missile weapons

| Weapon | Skill | Foot/Mtd | Damage | Range |
|--------|-------|----------|--------|-------|
| Crossbow, Light | Crossbow | Both (must be on foot to load) | 1D6+10 | Medium |
| Self Bow | Bow | Both | Character −1D6, **max 3D6** | Medium |
| Longbow *(Cambrians)* | Bow | Foot | Character −1D6, **max 4D6** | Long |
| Warbow *(Cambrians)* | Bow | Foot | Character −1D6, **max 5D6** | Long |

## Table 8.2 — Thrown weapons

| Weapon | Skill | Foot/Mtd | Damage | Range |
|--------|-------|----------|--------|-------|
| Dagger | Thrown Weapon | Both | Brawling | Short |
| Francisca | Thrown Weapon | Both | Character −1D6 (**regular Damage vs shielded opponents**) | Short |
| Javelin | Thrown Weapon | Both | Character −1D6, max 3D6 | Medium |
| Spear | Thrown Weapon | Both | Character −1D6 | Short |

## Notes

- **Longbow** — a hunting bow; only a wielder with the **Bow Skill and STR** draws it
  fully (others treat it as a self bow).
- **Warbow** — needs **Bow Skill and STR each ≥ 15** to draw fully (else a self bow).
- **Crossbow, Light** — a "one-foot" crossbow; braced with a foot in the stirrup to load.
- **Thrown dagger** — awkward in flight and unlikely to strike home (specialized throwing
  daggers appear later).
- A **spear** may be thrown but less effectively than a javelin (shorter range).
