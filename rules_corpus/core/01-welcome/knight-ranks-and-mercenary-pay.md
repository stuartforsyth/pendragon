Not all knights are equal. **About three in five** are mercenary or household knights; **almost two in five** are vassal knights holding land from the king or a great lord; **one in fifty** is a **banneret** — a lord without title who holds **at least ten other knights in vassalage**.

==A banneret's children do not inherit the rank.== It is earned in battle, and **only the king** may confer it, on his most trusted and valorous companions.

At **age 21** squires are free from service. Those destined for knighthood swear to uphold their duties and take an oath under a new lord. Squires who are heirs to property — usually first-born sons — become knights; younger offspring are not guaranteed the honor.

| **Leader** | None | Liege Lord | Liege Lord | Liege Lord |

## The Mercenary Knight

A hard life. The mercenary took the rank of knight but entered nobody's permanent service — typically a second-born whose elder sibling inherits everything, or the fourth or fifth child of a knight wealthy enough to confer the status.

He lives by his wits as a hired killer. If he has a servant it is **not usually a squire** (too extravagant) but a groom or lackey, who is not a battlefield follower. His equipment is rarely the latest type, his horses are smaller, and his clothes are often unfashionable or ragged. ==His kit deteriorates at **half its value per year**, and there is no liege lord to replace it.==

**The Indenture.** Mercenaries swear homage to no liege lord, only **temporary fealty**, committing to whichever commander offers a contract — an **indenture** specifying the term of service and rate of pay. It is written out and **cut in two halves along a zigzag**, one for the commander and one for the knight; if the terms are disputed the two pieces are matched to prove them originals.

## Table 1.2 — Mercenary Pay

| Pay rate | Foot Soldier | Archer | Mounted Cavalry | Knight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **d. per day** | ½ | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| **d. per month** | 10 | 20 | 40 | 80 |
| **d. per season** | 30 | 60 | 120 | 240 |
| **£ per year** | ½ | 1 | 2 | 4\* |

\* **Proven knights are paid more than unknown ones**, earning a bonus by Glory: **Notable** knights up to an extra **£1** per year, **Renowned** knights **£2**. On reaching **Illustrious** Glory a mercenary commands up to an extra **£4**, and his lord will often invite him to become a household knight.
