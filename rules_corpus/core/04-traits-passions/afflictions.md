Afflictions arise from certain failed Passion rolls (see the Passion Crisis). All can
be cured.

## Madness

A Maddened character is **Out of Game** — throws away weapons/armor, flees into the
wilderness (or collapses catatonic); the player hands the sheet to the GM and runs a
**Madness Solo** each Winter Phase while raving.

- **Glory:** 100 if it lasts a year or more, otherwise 25.
- **Curing:** only by someone who **Loves/Adores** the character, someone the character
  holds a Loyalty/Love/Adoration for, a **Famous/Exalted Merciful** person, a
  healing-professional monk/lady/hermit, or the **Holy Grail**. The healer succeeds a
  **Merciful** roll to calm them, then shelters and visits daily — **each month of care
  removes one year** of Madness duration; at 0 the mind clears (usually no memory of
  the episode). Madness lasting **under a month** must resolve on its own. Some
  enchanted wells/miracles/the Grail cure instantly.
- **Out-of-Game curing:** the GM may return the sheet at any time (adjusting Statistics
  to reflect the episode) — handy when the active character dies or goes Mad.

## Melancholy

A depressed state: **−5 to all Skill and Passion rolls**; incapable of normal action.

- **Glory:** 10. Effects wait until any current activity (e.g. a battle) finishes.
- **Value/duration:** equals a number of days (per Passion strength); left alone the
  **value drops 1/day**, then the character recovers. This value is the fixed
  opposition against a cure.
- **Friendly Cure:** someone with a Love/Loyalty/Adoration bond makes an **opposed
  Passion roll vs the Melancholy value** — win = lifted; partial = value −1, retry
  tomorrow; lose = the knight turns **violent** (→ Unfriendly Cure).
- **Unfriendly Cure:** being attacked snaps them out to defend themselves; combat (or a
  monk/lady's verbal assault/slap via a Cruel roll) continues until the healer is hurt
  or flees, or the Melancholic takes damage (which **ends** the affliction). One cure
  attempt per person per day.

## Misery

- **Becoming Miserable:** whenever an **Adoration** roll is failed (plus its other
  effects); or when Adoration is invoked away from the Beloved after Inspiration passes
  (if Impassioned via a critical, become **Melancholic** instead); the GM may also
  apply it on loss of a Love/Loyalty source. **Glory:** 10 (first time each year).
- **Effect:** **−2 to all Skills and Passions** until dispelled (stacks with other
  penalties).
- **Duration:** from a failed Adoration roll → ends 7 days after the following Sunday
  (or when cured); from loss of a loved one → weeks equal to the Passion's value.
- **Curing:** another person disturbs them with a successful **opposed Skill/Passion
  roll** (Intrigue, Orate; Merciful, Trusting; Homage, Loyalty (Companions), Chivalry,
  Duty (Vassals)) vs the causing Passion; a failed attempt triggers a
  Melancholy-style violent reaction. Also cured by seeing/hearing the Beloved.
