**Passions** are deeply-held beliefs/emotions that can inspire legendary feats or
coerce a character against their interests. They may grant temporary modifiers to
**Skills and Traits** — never to Characteristics or other Passions.

## Gaining a new Passion

Players may request one, or the GM may assign one when warranted (family murder →
Hate; rescue → Loyalty; miracle → Devotion). A new Passion starts at **1D6+6** (GM
may adjust). Too many Famous/Exalted Passions can cripple a knight (Lancelot's Love vs
Homage).

## Passion levels

- **Unsung** 1–4 · **Normal** 5–15 · **Famous** 16–20 · **Exalted** 20+x.
- A **Famous** Passion triggers easily (less autonomy); an **Exalted** Passion must
  always be acted upon (rare).

## Courts (40-point cap each)

Passions belong to four courts — **Fidelitas, Fervor, Adoratio, Civilitas** — plus
**Honor** (stands alone). **The sum of all Passions in a court may not exceed 40.**
Raising one Passion in a full court forces another in that court **down** (player's
choice), unless the rising one is already at 20.

**Directed Passions** are focused on a specific object (like Directed Traits).
Devotion (Deity) explicitly does **not** count as Directed.

## Raising & lowering

- **Raise:** gain a check and roll above the value in the Winter Phase, or add a
  Prestige Reward.
- **Lower (permanent):** a fumbled Passion roll (−1); a fumbled Passion Crisis roll
  (−1); a Prestige Reward; court-cap displacement; or **deliberately violating** the
  Passion.

**Violating a Passion:** to act against its reason (secretly meeting your lord's
enemy while holding Loyalty (Lord)) requires a **failed Passion roll** and reduces the
Passion by 1. Exalted Passions can't be failed this way unless modifiers drop them
below 20.

## Passions in battle

Each Passion may inspire **once per battle** (multi-day battles like Badon may allow
more). A knight does **not** get Homage/Loyalty (King) merely because their liege is
present — only if the liege is in **immediate danger**. When to roll **Hate** depends
on the enemy army's composition (vs a Saxon army, a Famous/Exalted Hate (Saxons) lets
the knight choose the round; vs Saxon mercenaries in a Cymric army, it must be rolled
on first contact).

**GM characters** generally don't use Passions early on, but should later in a
campaign and whenever a nameless knight defends their own home/liege.
