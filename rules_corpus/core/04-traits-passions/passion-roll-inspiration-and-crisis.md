Most Passion rolls are **unopposed**. Each Passion may be used to **provoke a bonus
only once per day** (rolls made purely to decide a course of action don't count
against this limit).

## Table 4.2 — Unopposed Passion Roll Results

| Result | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Critical** | **Impassioned** — gain a check |
| **Success** | **Inspired** — gain a check |
| **Failure** | **Passion Crisis** (see below) |
| **Fumble** | Lose 1 point from the Passion |

- **Impassioned:** **+10** to a single Skill or Trait (player's choice) until the
  evoking situation ends (a duel until someone drops; a battle Skill bonus lasts one
  Battle Turn; may modify a series of First Aid/Chirurgery rolls from one trauma).
- **Inspired:** **+5** to a single Skill or Trait while the situation persists.

**Modifying Traits with Passions:** the Inspired/Impassioned bonus can be applied to a
**Trait** to improve (or, by boosting an Unsung Trait, deliberately lower a Famous/
Exalted) Trait roll.

## Passion Crisis ("For Dole and Sorrow")

Triggered by a **failed** Table 4.2 roll — or when a character Inspired/Impassioned
fails spectacularly at the task, or the Passion's source is lost/removed forever.
Make an **unopposed roll against the triggering Passion** (+ modifiers):

- **Critical success → Madness** · **Success → Melancholy** · **Failure → no effect**
  · **Fumble → lose 1 point** from the Passion.

**Modifiers to that roll:** failed while Inspired **+0**; failed while Impassioned
**+5**; currently Inspired by it **+5**; crisis due to own action/inaction **+10**;
currently Impassioned by it **+10**.

**Affliction duration** (by unmodified Passion level):

| Level | Melancholic | Maddened |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Unsung (1–4) | 1D6 hours | one week |
| Normal (5–15) | one day | 1D6 weeks |
| Famous (16–20) | 1D6 days | 1D6 months |
| Exalted (20+x) | 1D6 weeks | 1D6 years |

(Both may be healed before the duration ends — see [[core.traits-passions.afflictions]].)

## Internal Conflict

Passions may be used in **opposed** rolls to resolve inner conflict — usually a
Passion vs a Trait. The player rolls both; the winner decides the action. (E.g. Hate
(Saxons) 18 vs Merciful 12 over sparing civilians.)
