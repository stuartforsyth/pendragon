Fidelitas is personal loyalty — always directed at a **person or group** (never a
place, action, animal, artifact, or ideal). Bonds are sealed by oaths; oath-breakers
become permanent outcasts.

- **Duty (Vassals)** — a lord's responsibility to their sworn knights; automatically
  gained whenever others swear Homage/Fealty to you. Invoked when a sworn vassal is in
  danger.
- **Fealty** — the "standard" feudal oath of obedience, sworn on Bible/relics, often
  **time-limited** and expiring without consequence; may be sworn to **multiple**
  lords; **inferior to Homage** (ignored if the two conflict). Mercenaries and
  professionals (stewards, clerks) swear Fealty for their term of service, then may
  serve anyone (even old enemies) without dishonor. Invoked when the lord is threatened
  or someone interferes with a deed done on his behalf.
- **Homage** — the **highest loyalty**, sworn for household membership or a land grant
  (heritable, if heirs also swear). One liege lord takes primacy over lesser homages.
  **Violating Homage is penalized more than any other loyalty — refusal loses Honor**
  (see [[core.aspirations.honor]]). Invoked when the liege or family is threatened,
  someone interferes with a deed for the lord, or the liege's Honor is impugned.
  - **Ending Homage/Fealty** never happens during a military expedition, only between
    wars: by *mutual agreement* (often generating a **Favor** to the releasing lord; a
    knight transferring up owes the new lord a Favor equal to the new Standard of
    Living); by *liege's choice* (needs serious cause; knight may demand trial by peers
    or combat); or by *knight's choice* (made public at court, returning gifts,
    declaring the lord's oath-violation; a resentful lord may name them enemy, granting
    **40 days' safe conduct**). A departing vassal keeps family lands but must quickly
    find a new liege or risk seizure.
- **Loyalty (Companions)** — mutual bond among a group of unrelated individuals (e.g. a
  band of PKs); list the members. Invoked when a companion is threatened, endangered,
  or fighting alongside.
- **Loyalty (King)** — loyalty to a king who isn't one's liege. If a vassal is more
  loyal to a king than their own lord, the lord may develop a **Jealousy** Obsession.
  It doesn't excuse feudal duty; escape requires formally breaking one's oath or being
  invited to serve the king directly. Invoked when the king/family is threatened,
  someone interferes with a deed for the king, or the king's Honor is impugned.
