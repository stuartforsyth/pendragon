## Directed Traits

A **Directed Trait** is a feeling focused on a person/group that overrides the general
attitude. Mark the Trait with an asterisk and note the object + modifier, e.g.
`*Suspicious (King Mark) +5`. When rolling that Trait about its object, add the
modifier **and reduce the opposing Trait by the same amount** (Trusting 14/Suspicious
6 becomes Trusting 9/Suspicious 11 vs King Mark).

- **Inherited:** starts at **half the parent's** value.
- **Voluntary:** usually **+1D6** (or as agreed).
- **GM-assigned:** usually **not more than +5**.
- **Raising:** with a check, roll higher than the current value in the Winter Phase to
  increase it by 1.

## Obsessions

A stronger Directed Trait that **always** affects actions. An **Obsession roll** is
made each time the character encounters or hears about its object. Obsessions arise
from unlucky rolls, GM ruling, or player request.

**Redemption:** (1) reduce it in the Winter Phase like a Passion; or (2) in the
object's presence, make an **opposed Honor vs Obsession** roll (Table 4.1). An
overweening Obsession can also be escaped by retiring to a monastery/hermitage
(Out of Game for good).

### Table 4.1 — Obsession Resolution Results (Honor vs Obsession)

| Result | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Critical | Overcome it — remove from sheet |
| Win | Partly overcome — **halve** its value |
| Tie | Attempt another opposed roll, or not (player's option) |
| Partial success | Reduce by 1 point |
| Loss | Waxes stronger — value **increases by half** |
| Fumble | Impossible to overcome — value **doubles** |

## The named Obsessions

- **Avarice** (extreme Selfish) — start **+2D6** to Selfish. Paths: fumble a Generous
  roll then succeed Selfish; **or** gain a Selfish critical bonus (GM picks the
  object). Effects: lacking the object causes **Misery** (once/day, crit or success →
  Misery); when a chance to obtain it arises, a Passion-style roll compels acquisition
  (critical = at any cost, morality aside).
- **Fear** (extreme Cowardly) — start **+1D6+5** to Cowardly. Paths: fumble a Valorous
  roll then succeed Cowardly; **or** GM/player decision. Effect: when confronted by the
  object, an unopposed Fear roll — success = refuse/withdraw to safety; critical = flee
  in terror at max rate until exhausted. Saves lives but costs Honor when fleeing is
  unbecoming.
- **Jealousy** (extreme Suspicious) — start **+2D6** to Suspicious. Paths include:
  fumble a Trusting roll then succeed Suspicious; fumble an obligatory Adoration roll;
  being publicly rebuffed by one's Adored (automatic); resentment of betters; Romantic
  Lovers must acquire a Jealousy of any threat to their Beloved. Effect: must try to
  **harm the Rival** (physically, socially, or via a Skill contest). In the Rival's
  company: **−5 to Forgiving, Just, Merciful, Prudent, Spiritual, and Trusting**.
