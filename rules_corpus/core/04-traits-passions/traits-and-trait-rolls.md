Traits are paired emotions (summing to 20) that influence Honor, Glory, and
behavior. A character tends to act on the higher Trait. **Normal (5–15)** Traits
allow the most freedom; **Famous (16–20)** and **Exalted (20+x)** Traits increasingly
compel behavior. A critical or fumble on a Trait roll may grant a **+5 to a
subsequent Skill or Passion roll**.

## Trait Roll results

Roll 1D20 against the Trait. Famous/Exalted Traits **must be rolled first**; an
unmodified Exalted Trait never fails.

- **Critical** — acts strongly per the Trait; take a check; GM may grant +5 to a
  later Skill/Passion roll.
- **Success** — acts per the Trait; check only if significant.
- **Failure** — roll again against the **Opposing Trait** (with modifiers). If that
  also fails, the player acts as they wish.
- **Fumble** — acts strongly per the **Opposing Trait** and checks it; GM may grant
  the +5.

**Opposing Trait roll:** Critical → acts strongly per opposing Trait (+check, +5);
Success → acts per opposing Trait; Failure → player's choice; Fumble → acts strongly
per the **original** Trait (+check, +5).

Modifiers apply to the Trait value (large/horrifying creatures often impose −Valorous;
this can drop an Exalted Trait below 20, forcing a roll).

## Trait Rolls in Play (three ways)

- **Decision rolls** — decide behavior. Not truly opposed: if the first roll
  succeeds, the second isn't made. Player picks which side to test first (except a
  Famous Trait must be tested first if acting against it; an Exalted Trait offers no
  choice).
- **Test rolls** — unopposed moral/spiritual trials (a *Just* roll to answer a justice
  riddle; an *Indulgent* roll to avoid getting drunk). Some tests just compare the
  **value** (Honest 15+ to reach Avalon; a shield only an Exaltedly Chaste knight may
  bear).
- **Game hint rolls** — GM prompts ("Suspicious characters get a roll here"); success
  = check, and an Opposing Trait roll follows a failure.

## Influencing another character

An **opposed Trait vs Trait** roll. If the influencer wins, the target takes an
automatic check in the influencing Trait; if the influencer loses, they make an
immediate Opposing Trait roll for a check. The winning Trait dictates the action; a
tie is no result. This can **diminish another's Famous/Exalted Trait**.

**Traits opposing Passions:** a Trait may oppose a Passion (own or another's) via an
opposed roll; the loser may make an Opposing Trait roll for a check.

## The Valorous Roll

A fairy or beast with a **Valorous Modifier** cannot be attacked until the character
makes a **Valorous roll** (a Test Roll — everyone rolls, even Unsung/Normal),
adjusted by the modifier, on sighting it. **Fail** → hesitate one Combat Round, may
retry. **Fumble** → flee the scene.

## Religious Virtues (16+ in all six → Religious Knight boon, p.127)

- **Christian:** Chaste, Forgiving, Merciful, Modest, Spiritual, Temperate
- **Pagan:** Energetic, Generous, Honest, Lustful, Proud, Spiritual
- **Wodinic:** Generous, Indulgent, Proud, Reckless, Valorous, Worldly
- **Heathen:** Arbitrary, Honest, Proud, Spiritual, Suspicious, Vengeful
