Characters heal only through **natural healing**, **Chirurgery**, **First Aid**, or
magic/miracle. Weekly healing conventionally occurs **Sunday at noon**; insisting on
activity instead of rest triggers **Aggravation**.

## Natural Healing

Restores **Healing Rate** (= CON/5) HP per week. Deterioration and Aggravation may make a
week's net result nil or a loss.

## Chirurgery (for Debilitated characters — halts Deterioration)

One roll per week (Sunday noon). **−5** in unclean/unhealthy conditions.

- **Critical:** no Deterioration, **and** add the Healing Rate to current HP now.
- **Success:** no Deterioration.
- **Failure:** Deterioration occurs.
- **Fumble:** Deterioration occurs **and** lose 1D6 HP.

## First Aid (emergency, per wound, fresh wounds < 1 day)

Each wound takes ≥ 5 minutes (not during combat). A **failed** attempt bars any further
First Aid on that wound. First Aid on Major/Mortal wounds does **not** clear the
Debilitated state. May be attempted **on oneself at −10**; a squire may attempt it if no
PKs are present.

- **Critical:** regain **double Healing Rate** (up to the wound's value).
- **Success:** regain **Healing Rate** (up to the wound's value).
- **Failure:** no improvement.
- **Fumble:** lose **1D3 HP** directly and become **Debilitated**.
