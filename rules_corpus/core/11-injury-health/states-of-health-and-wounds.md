A character is either **Healthy** or **Debilitated** (no middle ground). Healthy
characters need no care and heal weekly at their Healing Rate, even below full HP.

**The Debilitated box is checked when a character:** drops below the Unconscious
Characteristic; takes a Major Wound; survives a Mortal Wound; fumbles a First Aid roll;
or contracts illness/poison. It stays checked until a **successful Chirurgery roll** and
recovery to **half Total HP**.

## Wounds

Each hit is a separate **wound** = damage dealt minus Armor (and Shield/Parry)
Protection; record each separately (First Aid is per-wound). Three types by the size of a
**single** blow relative to CON:

- **Minor Wound** — below the Major Wound value (= CON). Painful but not disabling
  (several can still drop you below Unconscious).
- **Major Wound** — ≥ CON from one blow. The character **immediately goes unconscious**,
  checks Debilitated, and rolls once on **Table 11.1: Characteristic Lost** (lose 1 point,
  recompute derived stats). Any action before it fully heals causes Aggravation.
- **Mortal Wound** — ≥ Total Hit Points from one blow; usually kills. Survives only if
  **First Aid within one hour** restores them to ≥1 HP (Chirurgery can't help until
  then); then roll **three times** on Table 11.1.

### Table 11.1 — Characteristic Lost (1D6)

| 1D6 | Characteristic |
|-----|----------------|
| 1 | SIZ |
| 2 | DEX |
| 3 | STR |
| 4 | CON |
| 5 | APP |
| 6 | none |

## Unconscious

A character passes out at **HP ≤ Unconscious** or on any Major Wound (they may attempt
an **Emergency Dismount** first). Check Debilitated. They may have brief lucid moments
(whisper, dictate a will). Recovering above Unconscious or a successful Chirurgery
restores faculties (though still Debilitated if applicable).

**Fighting beyond debilitation:** to keep fighting past Unconscious / after a Major
Wound, make an unopposed **Energetic** roll **and** a roll vs **current HP** — both must
succeed. Each round of fighting on costs **1 Aggravation** (acting with slow deliberation
= −10 to all Skills but less Aggravation).

## Near Death

At **0 or negative HP** (any cause), the character dies at **midnight** unless **First
Aid** restores them to positive HP that day (then Chirurgery is needed). The GM may rule
that HP more negative than can be healed = instant death.
