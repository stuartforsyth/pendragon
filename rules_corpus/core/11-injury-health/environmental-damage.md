## Cold

Unopposed **CON roll**; **failure/fumble = 1D6** taken directly from HP (no wound). Roll
**once per day** in cold, **every round** in freezing (e.g. fording an ice-choked river).
Chirurgery restores **half** the cold points (**all** on a critical).

## Disease

Rare (usually from a deep dungeon). GM sets the damage (usually Characteristic/HP loss,
no wound). A diseased character is **Debilitated**.

## Falling

<2 yards = no damage. **2 yards = 1D6**, +1D6 per additional 2 yards, **max 16D6**.
**Armor does not absorb** falling damage. A **Horsemanship** roll (falling from a horse)
or **DEX** roll (otherwise) **removes 1D6** dice (2D6 on a critical); a **fumble doubles**
the dice. Record as one wound.

## Falling objects

Small (palm-sized rock) **2D6**; boulders/masonry **3D6+**, up to **16D6**. **Armor
protects normally**; an aware character may add **Shield** Protection (not Parry). Each is
a separate wound.

## Fire & Heat

**1D6 per Combat Round.** Armor protects for **only one round**, then is too hot until a
one-round break to cool. Great heat (wyrm's breath) may do **2D6+** or ignore armor. All
fire damage is a **single wound**; one First Aid roll per instance.

## Suffocation (water, smoke, vapors)

Unopposed **CON roll each round** (holding breath); once failed, **1D6/round** while
exposed (more if toxic). A **cumulative −1 to CON per round** beyond the first (7th round
= −6). No wounds; recovery from near-drowning may be rapid (GM option).

## Poison

Every poison has a **Potency**. On introduction, make an **opposed CON roll vs the fixed
Potency** (Fixed Opposition).

### Table 11.3 — Poisoning Results

| CON result | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| Win | No effect |
| Partial success | Lose **half** the Potency from CON |
| Loss | Lose the **full** Potency from CON |

**Sample potencies:** Foxglove 5 · Dwale (Belladonna) 10 · Hemlock 15 · Arsenic 20 ·
Wolfsbane/Monkshood 25. **CON 0 = death** (recompute derived stats as CON drops). Lost
CON returns at the **Healing Rate per week**.

**Antidotes:** a successful **Chirurgery** roll provides one (ingested then vomited),
before CON hits 0. Unicorn horn, emerald powder, bezoars, and toadstones are believed to
cancel poison; a **taster** is the surest prevention.
