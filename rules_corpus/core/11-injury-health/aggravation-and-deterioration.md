## Aggravation — worsening a condition through activity

Applied by incident (GM judgment), not weekly. Characters **below the Unconscious level
cannot take Aggravation** (they can only rest). **Chirurgery cannot halt Aggravation.**

### Table 11.2 — Activity and Aggravation

| Activity | Examples | Healthy | Debilitated |
|----------|----------|---------|-------------|
| **No activity** | rest, eat sensibly, sleep | None (best recovery) | None (best recovery) |
| **Light** | short walks, conversation, short letters | None | None unless overdone |
| **Moderate** | longer walks, riding (even briefly), dancing, romance, arguing | None | Possible (GM option) |
| **Strenuous** | fighting, running, climbing, significant travel | Possible if many small wounds or below half HP | **Certain** |

## Deterioration — untended wounds worsening

**1D6 damage per week** to **Debilitated** characters who do **not** get a successful
Chirurgery roll that week (no wound recorded; First Aid can't help). It occurs
**immediately before** the weekly natural Healing Rate is applied. Only one Chirurgery
roll is allowed per week (Deterioration hits on a failure/fumble).
