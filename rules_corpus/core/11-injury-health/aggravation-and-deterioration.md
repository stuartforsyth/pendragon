## Aggravation — worsening a condition through activity

Making a condition worse through activity while ill or injured.

**Effect: 1 or more points of damage subtracted from current Hit Points, with no wound recorded.** **One point is the normal loss**; a character may suffer **up to 3** from extremely violent or sustained activity (the book's example is mortal combat with an ogre) at the GM's option. **First Aid cannot cure or reduce Aggravation damage.**

Aggravation damage occurs **after the activity is complete**, unless the GM decides otherwise — so a character may **lose consciousness immediately after the rash action** that earned it.

Applied by incident (GM judgment), not weekly; current health, the extent of injuries, and the duration and intensity of the activity all matter. Aggravation is possible for **any injured character, Healthy or Debilitated**, undertaking **strenuous** activity; **Debilitated characters may suffer it on even moderate activity**. Characters **below the Unconscious level cannot take Aggravation** — they can perform only light activity and are normally comatose. **Chirurgery cannot halt Aggravation.**

For the per-round case — fighting on past Unconscious or after a Major Wound, which costs **1 Aggravation per round** — see [[core.injury-health.states-of-health-and-wounds]].

### Table 11.2 — Activity and Aggravation

| Activity | Examples | Healthy | Debilitated |
|----------|----------|---------|-------------|
| **No activity** | rest, eat sensibly, sleep | None (best recovery) | None (best recovery) |
| **Light** | short walks, conversation, short letters | None | None unless overdone |
| **Moderate** | longer walks, riding (even briefly), dancing, romance, arguing | None | Possible (GM option) |
| **Strenuous** | fighting, running, climbing, significant travel | Possible if many small wounds or below half HP | **Certain** |

## Deterioration — untended wounds worsening

**1D6 damage per week** to **Debilitated** characters who do **not** get a successful Chirurgery roll that week (no wound recorded; First Aid can't help). It occurs **immediately before** the weekly natural Healing Rate is applied. Only one Chirurgery roll is allowed per week (Deterioration hits on a failure/fumble).
