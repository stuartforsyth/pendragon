## Control Mount (Act) — Mounted

Get an unruly mount under control before another Action. See "Controlling Horses
in Combat" (p. 181).
- **Contest:** Unopposed Horsemanship roll (**−5** for non-combat-trained horses;
  further penalty equal to Hit Points the horse has lost to wounds).

A horse not trained for combat (any from Table 10.3: Riding Horses) forces a
Horsemanship roll at **−5** at the start of each round: **success** = control +
another action this round; **critical** = controlled for the rest of combat
unless wounded; **failure** = no other action this round; **fumble** = thrown,
must make an Emergency Dismount (p. 150). When any horse takes **5+ points** of
damage in a round, check injury effects; thereafter roll Horsemanship each round
(penalty = total HP lost) before acting — a combat-trained horse settles on a
critical *or* regular success.

## Mount Up (Act) — Mounted, Unmounted

Takes one Combat Round to mount/dismount, unless a squire holds the reins, or you
roll Movement Rate (capped by Horsemanship) to leap into/out of the saddle and
act the same round. Mounting under blows uses an opposed roll (capped by
Horsemanship) vs. the opponent's Action.
- **Win:** mount up, take no damage.
- **Partial Success:** mount up but take damage; no shield/parry protection.
- **Loss:** remain unmounted and take damage; no shield/parry protection.
- **Fumble:** fall; start next round on the ground.
