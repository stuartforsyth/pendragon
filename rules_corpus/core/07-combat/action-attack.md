## Attack (Combat) — Mounted, Unmounted

The most common Action: try to damage the opponent with any Weapon Skill.
- **Distance:** Close Quarters, Normal, Long, Missile
- **Contest:** Weapon Skill vs. opponent's Action.

**Attacking a Horse or Weapon.** You may declare an attack on a rider's horse,
though this is dishonorable between knights (**−1 Honor**); a partial success by
the opponent lets the horse benefit from Shield/Parry Protection. Attacking a
horse from foot negates the −5 Height penalty, but the mounted opponent still
gets their +5 Height Advantage. You may instead target the opponent's **weapon**
to break it: the small target imposes **−5** to Weapon Skill, and a win breaks it
(a sword is knocked from the hand instead).

## Reckless Attack (Combat) — Mounted, Unmounted

An unguarded frenzy; always earns a **check to the Reckless trait**. Famously
Reckless characters must use it unless Prudent wins an opposed roll; Exemplary
Reckless must always use it. If one side Reckless-Attacks and the other Defends,
the two cancel and combat resolves normally.
- **Distance:** Close Quarters, Normal, Long
- **Contest:** Weapon Skill vs. opponent's Action; if the opponent is rolling a
  Weapon Skill they get **+5**. **Neither** side may apply Shield/Parry Protection.
- **Win:** deal **+2D6** Damage (**+6D6** on a critical).

## Withhold Damage (Combat) — Mounted, Unmounted

Deliberately reduce your strike: either withhold a preset number of dice, or use
the flat/butt for half damage (roll normal, halve). Using the flat every blow
dishonors the opponent (**−1 Honor** at end of combat). Only usable with a
normal Attack — never on a Mounted Charge or with thrown/missile weapons.
- **Distance:** Close, Normal, Long — **Contest:** Weapon Skill vs. opponent's Action.
