Disarm attempts to knock away the opponent's weapon or object from their grasp.
(Attempts to *wrench or seize and take* a weapon use the **Grapple** Action
instead.)
- **Distance:** Close Quarters, Normal
- **Contest:** Weapon Skill vs. opponent's Action
- **Critical:** the weapon/object flies out of easy reach, or lands at the
  character's feet (character's choice).
- **Win:** the opponent drops the weapon/object within reach; it may be picked up
  as a non-Combat action the following round. If more than one character tries to
  pick it up, resolve with an opposed DEX roll.
