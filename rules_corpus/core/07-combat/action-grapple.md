## Grapple (Brawling) — Mounted, Unmounted

A normally two-handed attempt to grab a foe or seize an object from their grip
(one-handed at **−5**). A foot character may Grapple a foe on foot or mounted,
but a mounted character may only Grapple another mounted foe. You may Grapple
**only one** foe at a time.
- **Distance:** Contact or Close Quarters
- **Contest:** Brawling vs. opponent's Action. **On a win, select a Follow-up
  Action from Table 7.3.**

## Follow-up Actions

A successful Grapple can be maintained into later rounds (no re-roll needed unless
the opponent attempts an Escape). Roll your normal Damage dice (**+2D6** on a
critical) and compare the total against the opponent's listed Characteristic/Skill
— the total must **exceed** the target for the action to take effect. This
"damage" ignores armor and normally inflicts no actual damage, **except** if the
roll is **≥ twice the target**, you also inflict your Brawling Damage (ignoring
armor).

**Table 7.3 — Follow-up Actions** (damage must exceed opponent's…):

| Action | Beat | Outcome | Grapple maintained? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disarm | Weapon Skill | Opponent drops weapon | Y |
| Hold | STR | Opponent pinned | Y |
| Lift | SIZ | Opponent lifted into the air | Y |
| Seize Weapon | Weapon Skill + ½ STR | Character takes opponent's weapon | N |
| Tackle | DEX | Opponent taken to the ground & pinned (mounted: 1D6 fall damage) | Y |
| Throw Down | SIZ | Must be Lifted first; thrown for 1D6 (foot) or 2D6 (horseback) damage | N |

Notes: a **Disarm** critical sends the weapon out of reach. A **pinned** opponent
(Hold/Tackle) may only attempt Escape, at **−10/+10**; the grappler may then use
any Follow-up automatically. SIZ ≥ 5× the opponent pins one-handed at no penalty;
SIZ < ⅕ the opponent cannot pin at all. A **Lifted** foe can be carried off
across a horse (lifting a foot foe from the saddle needs character+mount SIZ ≥
1½× the target). **Tackles** between mounted combatants require a Horsemanship
roll to avoid taking fall damage yourself.

## Escape (Act) — Unmounted

A pinned character may try to escape on later rounds: opposed roll of your
**Brawling −10** vs. the opponent's **Brawling +10**. Win → roll "damage" trying
to beat the grappler's STR. Lose → the opponent may execute a Follow-up Action as
normal.
