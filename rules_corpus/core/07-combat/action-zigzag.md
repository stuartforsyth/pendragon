Zigzag is an evasive Action used while moving, effective **only against missile
attacks** (use Dodge for melee). A combatant wearing **no armor gets +5**. You
cannot Zigzag while moving faster than Walking pace.
- **Distance:** Missile
- **Contest:** unopposed Movement Rate roll, made **before** the missile attack.
- **Critical:** opponent takes **−10** to Missile Weapon Skill; you move up to
  full Movement Rate.
- **Success:** opponent takes **−5**; you move up to half Movement Rate.
- **Failure:** no modifier; you move up to half Movement Rate.
- **Fumble:** no modifier; you stumble and lose all Movement Rate this round.
