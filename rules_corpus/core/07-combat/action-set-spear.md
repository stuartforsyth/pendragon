Set Spear counters a declared or potential **Charge** attack. The character sets
the butt of the spear against or into the ground, angled at the opponent or their
mount. The spear may be one- or two-handed: one hand allows use of a shield;
a **two-handed grip adds +2D6 damage**.

This Action **only works against an opponent using the Charge Skill** — otherwise
it counts as a simple Attack.
- **Distance:** Long
- **Contest:** Spear Skill vs. opponent's Action
