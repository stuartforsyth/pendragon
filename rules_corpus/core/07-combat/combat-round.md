The **Combat Round** is the basic time measure for single or small-group combat:
a short, elastic unit — the time to conceive of and perform one action in melee.
Rounds continue in succession until everyone is done fighting (through
incapacitation, death, surrender, or flight).

Each character is limited to **one action per round**. This is a loose limit: a
round is not merely a single blow but an exchange of attacks, parries, and
maneuvering. A roll made as a *reaction* to another (e.g. the DEX roll when your
Knockdown value is exceeded) is a **free action** and does not count as your
action for the round.

**Combat Round procedure** — both combatants simultaneously follow, each round:

1. **Declare Combat Actions**
2. **Resolve Combat Actions**
3. **Winner's Outcome**
4. **Loser's Outcome**
5. **Combat Movement**

**Step One — Declare.** All combatants state what they intend to do, the weapon
they wield, and their targets/opponents. Combatants within one yard of each
other are automatically **engaged** and need not move to fight. If timing
matters (e.g. one sets a spear while the other makes a Mounted Charge), resolve
conflicting declarations with opposed DEX rolls — declarations are made from
**lowest DEX to highest**, and the winner resolves first in Step Two.

**Step Two — Resolve.** Any Skill/Combat Skill resolution is made now.
Characters can either fight *or* move, not both — except a **Mounted Charge**,
which combines movement and attack and is resolved here (splitting into two
actions halves the Skill used in each). If combat occurs, both combatants roll
their modified Combat Skills using opposed resolution and consult **Table 7.1:
Combat Action Resolution Results**. Some actions (e.g. Dodge, Disarm) supersede
that table, as noted in the action's definition.
