A Mounted Charge uses a weapon at full gallop. You must ride a combat-trained
horse capable of Charge Damage, not be engaged at the start of the round, and aim
at a specific opponent. Any weapon may be used except one causing Brawling
damage; spears and lances get special bonuses. The charge **must continue in a
Pass-by** regardless of hit or miss (see p. 149).

- **Distance:** Special — at least **six yards apart per charging character**.
- **Contest:** Charge vs. opponent's Action. **+5** if armed with a **couched**
  spear or lance against anyone not using a Long-reach weapon. For any other
  weapon, effective Charge Skill is the **lower** of Charge or the relevant
  Weapon Skill; and effective Charge Skill can **never exceed Horsemanship**.

**Results** (in addition to / instead of Table 7.1):
- **Win:** hit for Weapon Damage **+1D6**, or the horse's **Charge Damage** if
  using a couched spear/lance. Except on a critical, damage dice may not exceed
  the horse's Charge Damage. A critical adds **+4D6**.
- **Partial Success:** a spear, lance, or hafted weapon breaks.
- **Tie:** hafted weapon breaks; if both critical, the horses crash, each taking
  the other's Charge Damage.
- **Fumble:** sword dropped / hafted weapon breaks; a couched spear or lance also
  breaks **and** triggers a Horsemanship roll on **Table 7.2**.

**Charge Opportunities** (large melee, not engaged at round start): roll 1D6 —
**1–3** may Charge a random opponent (if that opponent is also mounted on a
combat horse, roll again to see if they also Charge); **4–6** no opportunity
(engage in normal melee instead).

**Table 7.2 — Mounted Lance Charge Fumble** (roll Horsemanship):
- **Critical:** lance broken; no further ill effects.
- **Success:** lance broken; struck by flying splinters for **1D6** (armor does
  not protect).
- **Failure:** tip buries in the earth and launches you from the saddle; lance
  broken, **2D6** damage (armor does not protect).
- **Fumble:** tip buries and brings down both you and horse; lance broken,
  **3D6** damage (armor does not protect).
