Step Two (opposed Combat Skill rolls, read on **Table 7.1**) leaves a **winner and a loser**, a **tie** (both strike — each is the other's "loser"), or **two losers** (nobody is hurt). Then:

## Step Three — Winner's Outcome

The winner rolls dice equal to their **Damage Characteristic** (or their **horse's Damage** when charging with a lance or spear), modified by weapon type and grip where applicable. A **critical success adds +4D6** to the Damage for this round (e.g. 5D6 becomes 5D6+4D6). A notable success (a critical, a success against a noteworthy foe, or one that moves the story on) may earn an experience check for the weapon used, with GM approval. The winner then goes to Step Five (Combat Movement).

## Step Four — Loser's Outcome

The loser **takes the winner's damage**, then checks, **in this order**:

1. **Knockdown** — the *base* damage (before armor, rebating or other reductions) against their Knockdown (= SIZ). Greater → unopposed DEX roll (success/critical: stays up; failure/fumble: knocked down); greater than twice → automatically knocked down. See core.combat.knockdown.
2. **Dropped/Broken Weapon** — a loser who **fumbled** drops their sword or breaks a non-sword weapon; swords break non-sword weapons (not daggers) on a tie, and break any weapon when the loser fumbles and the winner criticals. See core.combat.dropped-broken-weapon.
3. **Armor, Shield, and Parry Protection** — damage taken = winner's damage total − Armor Protection; if the loser rolled a **partial success**, also subtract their Shield or Parry Protection. See core.combat.protection.

Then record the wound and check its type: actual damage ≥ CON is a **Major Wound** (unconscious); Current Hit Points below the Unconscious value → unconscious; 0 or less → mortally wounded. See core.combat.damage-and-wounds.

Note: losing the opposed roll does **not** by itself knock the loser down — knockdown comes only from the damage-vs-SIZ check above.
