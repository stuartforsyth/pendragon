Circumstances apply bonuses or penalties. Against a single foe all modifiers
always apply; against multiple foes, different modifiers may apply per opponent.
**All modifiers are cumulative.**

- **Cover** — an obstacle (tree, bush, low wall) partially protecting a character
  in melee gives **both** combatants **−5**.
- **Height Advantage** — fighting from height (horseman vs. foot, rampart vs.
  climber, standing vs. prone) gives a **+5/−5 reflexive** modifier in the taller
  combatant's favor. Negated if an unmounted combatant uses a two-handed spear or
  halberd against a mounted foe. A mounted combatant using any weapon **except a
  spear cannot strike a prone opponent**. Missile attacks do not benefit.
- **Strike from Behind** — when the opponent doesn't know an attack is coming:
  an unopposed Attack at **+5** (usually dishonorable; may cost Honor).
- **Immobile Target** — grappled, entangled, stuck, etc.: **−10/+10 reflexive**
  to all actions needing freedom of movement.
- **Surprise** — a wholly unprepared opponent grants the attacker **+5** to
  Weapon Skill for the first round. A missile attack from Surprise ignores the
  target's shield penalty.
- **Visibility** — darkness, fog, or smoke penalizes all Combat Actions (see the
  Awareness skill, p. 98).
