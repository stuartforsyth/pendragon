A Player may declare a Self-sacrifice for the common good (**GM approval
required** to prevent petty use or the loss of key characters like King Arthur).
The player describes their objective and desired outcome, which **succeeds
automatically** — no dice. In return, **the character dies at the end of the
action** and receives **1,000 Glory** for a heroic death, plus normal Glory for
deeds later witnessed or verified.

Limitations:
- It is not carte blanche — the GM maintains drama (e.g. a knight holding a
  bridge buys time, but not enough for the others to leisurely stroll away). The
  GM decides how long pursuers are delayed and need not tell the players.
- Against a **gargantuan** enemy (dragon, huge giant) a kill is **not
  guaranteed**: instead the hero does the equivalent of a **critical success with
  maximum damage, bypassing the foe's armor** (e.g. 4D6 +4D6 crit at max = 48
  points).
