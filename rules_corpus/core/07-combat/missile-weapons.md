Knights consider using missile weapons against other knights **cowardly** — a
knight gets a **Cowardly check and loses 1 Honor** for using a bow or crossbow
against another knight in single combat. (Crossbows in a besieged garrison are
acceptable.)

**Missile Ranges & penalties:**
- **Short** — up to 30 yards; the max range for all thrown weapons except javelins.
- **Medium** — 31–100 yards; **−5** to Weapon Skill; max range for javelins.
- **Long** — 101–200 yards; **−10** to Weapon Skill.

**Shielded Targets.** A shield-bearer aware of being targeted imposes the
shield's **Missile Attack Penalty** (e.g. kite shield **−10**) on the archer. The
archer may apply it to shots at the **mount** instead, but loses their own
shield's benefit that round. Shield/Parry Protection points otherwise do not
apply to missile fire.

**Cover** (targets on foot behind cover): Light **−5** (brush, moderate rocks),
Moderate **−10** (low wall, dense trees), Significant **−15** (arrow slit).

**Other Modifiers:** stationary opponents reduce range penalties by 5; Zigzag
**−5** (or **−10**); Surprise **+5**; wind — light 0, irregular **−5**, strong
constant **−10** (wind does not affect hand-hurled weapons).

**Closing Distance (Table 7.5)** — rounds to cross each band; compare to the
weapon's rate of discharge for shots before the attacker arrives. Zigzagging
attackers always use Walking Rate.

| Pace | Short (≤30) | Medium (31–100) | Long (101–200) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walking | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Jogging | 1 | 2 | 4 |
| Running | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Walk (mount) | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Trot | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Canter | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Gallop | 1 | 1 | 1 |

**Other considerations:** *Loosing into a Melee* — equal chance to hit every
combatant; the GM assigns the target at random. *Mounted Targets* — roll 1D6:
**1–4** hits the mount, **5–6** the rider. *Visibility* — adverse conditions may
require an Awareness roll to see the target before shooting. *Water* — a wet bow
or crossbow gives **−5** Weapon Skill, **doubles** all effective ranges, and
**halves** damage.
