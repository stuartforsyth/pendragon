title: Damage & Wounds (Loser's Outcome)

---

Record damage taken (after subtracting Protection) in the Wounds section of the
character sheet and subtract it from Total Hit Points. Note each wound
**separately** — First Aid is applied to each individually — and check what type
was inflicted (Minor, Major, or Mortal).

- **Major Wound:** if actual damage **equals or exceeds the loser's CON**, they
  suffer a Major Wound and fall unconscious.
- **Unconscious:** if Current Hit Points fall **below the Unconscious
  Characteristic**, the loser falls unconscious. They are out of the fight until
  First Aid raises Current Hit Points to at least their Unconscious value.
- **Mortally Wounded:** if Hit Points drop to **0 or less**, the character will
  die if not quickly rescued.

If **mounted** when knocked unconscious or Major-Wounded, the character may
attempt an **Emergency Dismount** (unopposed DEX roll): success = a gentle fall
doing little or no damage; failure = a bad fall causing **1D6** damage.

See *Chapter 11: Injury and Health* for wound effects and recovery.
