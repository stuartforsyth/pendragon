Squires stay near their knights but may not be close enough to help immediately.
Declare **why** you are calling — usually one of: **First Aid** (heal wounds),
**New weapon** (brings a requested weapon; the knight re-arms), or **Fresh
horse** (brings a mount). Calling does not instantly accomplish the action —
e.g. First Aid needs the squire to pull the knight from combat and spend several
(**1D6**) minutes treating, possibly removing armor.
- **Distance:** Within hearing range
- **Contest:** None — make an unopposed **Squire Roll** (target = Squire Skill).

**Squire Roll Result:**
- **Critical:** squire acts and proceeds with the desired action **this round**.
- **Success:** squire acts and proceeds **next round**.
- **Fail:** squire does not act; the character may call again next round.
- **Fumble:** squire is nowhere to be seen; may reappear later at the GM's discretion.
