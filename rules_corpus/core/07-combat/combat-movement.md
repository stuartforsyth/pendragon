Characters who declared movement **other than a charge** now simultaneously move
up to their Movement Rate in yards. A character need not move their full rate,
but **once they choose to stop, they cannot move further** that round.

Movement decides whether a character has closed with an enemy so they may
engage. Once two combatants are within one yard of each other, melee combat is
possible. Except for charges, **all movement takes place at the end of the
Combat Round**.
