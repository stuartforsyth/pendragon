Pendragon centers on the mounted knight. See also the Control Mount, Mount Up,
Mounted Charge, Trample, and Zigzag actions.

**Horsemanship Limitation.** Horsemanship limits the **base value** of all Weapon
Skills used while riding; modifiers (combat, Passions, etc.) still apply and can
push the effective value back above Horsemanship. *E.g. Sword 17 with
Horsemanship 15 → effective 15 while mounted; a +5 mounted-vs-foot modifier then
raises it to 20 against that foe.*

**Pass-by.** A Mounted Charge always overshoots the target by a Pass-by distance
(the horse's motion after impact). Minimum distance to rein in = **half** the
horse's normal Movement Rate (the rider may circle to start next round in Normal
range); maximum = the horse's **gallop** Movement Rate minus the distance to
reach the target. On a Win or mutual miss the rider sets the distance; on a Tie
(double success), Partial, or Loss the maximum is **halved and the rider must go
the full distance**. A dismounted rider's horse gallops on for the maximum; if
the horse is knocked down or falls, no Pass-by occurs.

**Mounted Knockdown.** Normal Knockdown rules apply: total damage over SIZ → DEX
roll to stay in the saddle; over **twice SIZ** → automatically thrown. Falling
from the saddle causes **1D6** damage.

**Horse and Rider Knocked Down.** If total damage to *either* horse or rider
exceeds the **horse's SIZ**, the horse makes a DEX roll to stay standing; failure
knocks it down. The rider may attempt an Emergency Dismount; if that also fails,
the rider is **crushed** by the falling steed for the horse's normal Damage
(armor protects normally).

**Emergency Dismount** (unopposed Horsemanship roll — **Table 7.4**):
- **Critical:** land on feet, upright next round, no damage, keep all weapons.
- **Success:** land on feet, upright next round, no damage, but **drop** held weapons.
- **Failure:** improper dismount, **1D6** damage, drop all weapons.
- **Fumble:** **2D6** damage, drop all weapons; if the horse fell, it lands on
  the rider for the horse's Damage.

**Escaping from Under a Fallen Horse.** Each round roll the rider's **STR vs. the
horse's SIZ**; each helper adds +5 to the strongest person's STR (who rolls).

**Limited Two-handed Mounted Weapons.** From horseback, only a **longsword** and
a **two-handed spear** may be used two-handed; other two-handed weapons are too
unbalanced and risk unhorsing the rider.

**Too Big for Your Horse.** Compare the horse's STR to the rider's **SIZ×2**
(subtract 5 if unarmored). If that total exceeds the horse's STR: **−5**
Horsemanship, **−5** horse Movement Rate, treated as Forced March on long travel,
and horse Damage **−1D6**. If the rider's SIZ equals the horse's STR, the horse
cannot bear them at all.
