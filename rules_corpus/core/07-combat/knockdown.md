Whenever a character takes a blow (from a weapon or another source, such as
riding into a tree limb), the **base damage** — damage before armor, rebated
weapons, or other reductions — is compared to their **Knockdown value**, which
equals their **SIZ** Characteristic.

- If damage received is **greater than Knockdown**, the character makes an
  unopposed **DEX roll** (whether on foot or mounted); multiple knockdowns in a
  round each require their own roll.
  - **Critical or success:** stays upright / in the saddle, no penalty.
  - **Failure or fumble:** knocked down — begins next round on the ground. If
    mounted, suffers **1D6** damage from the fall and drops their weapon.
- If damage is **greater than twice Knockdown**, the character is
  **automatically** knocked down (mounted: fall, 1D6 damage, drop weapon).
- If damage is **greater than the horse's SIZ**, the horse may be knocked down
  too — see "Horse and Rider Knocked Down" (p. 150).
