title: Armor, Shield, and Parry Protection
see_also: [core.combat.damage-and-wounds, core.combat.winners-outcome]
---

Armor and shields absorb damage; parries block it. All are expressed as a
**Protection value**.

The actual damage the loser takes is the difference between the **winner's
damage total** and the loser's **Armor Protection value**.

If the loser rolled a **partial success**, they may *also* apply the **Shield or
Parry Protection value** of their shield/weapon on top of Armor.
