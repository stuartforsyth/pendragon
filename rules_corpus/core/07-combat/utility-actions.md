## Claim a Prisoner (Act) — Mounted, Unmounted

It takes a full Combat Round to accept a foe's surrender, during which you may
take no other action.

## Pick Up (Act) — Mounted, Unmounted

Retrieve something within reach not worn by/attached to another person or object.
Use your hands, or use a spear/lance to pick up small objects from horseback.
- **Distance:** Contact, Close Quarters, Normal
- **Contest:** Unopposed = automatic success; if opposed, roll **DEX+5** vs.
  opponent's Action.

## Don Armor (Act) — Unmounted

Putting on armor takes more than one round; the time depends on armor type and
whether a squire/knight assists (see "Armoring Up", p. 169). Each round donning
armor must be devoted to nothing else. If forced to fight before finishing, you
count as **unarmored**.

## Study (Act) — Mounted, Unmounted

Look over the combat area to see who is prone, wounded, or faring well; the GM
describes the scene, with more detail the more rounds you spend. Obscured things
may require an immediate Awareness roll (with penalties). If attacked while
Studying, you may cancel it for another Action at **−5** for the first round.
