**Brawling Skill** applies whenever a character fights unarmed or with an
improvised weapon (e.g. a musical instrument) or small weapon (knife, dagger). It
always inflicts the character's **Brawling Damage**, though the weapon/object may
modify this. Brawling also covers grapples, slams, and escapes (see separate
entries).

## Barehand Attack (Brawling) — Mounted, Unmounted

Functions like Attack but uses the **Brawling Skill** instead of a Weapon Skill;
also used for daggers/improvised weapons. Unarmed strikes are a **punch** (closed
fist) or **slap** (open hand).
- **Distance:** Contact or Close Quarters
- **Contest:** Brawling vs. opponent's Action
- **Win:** Brawling Damage for a punch/brawling weapon, or half for a slap; a
  **critical adds +2D6**. Weapons inflict Brawling Damage + their bonus, and add
  the normal **+4D6** on a critical.
- **Fumble:** stumble (**−5** next round); improvised/wooden weapons break;
  daggers are dropped.

## Slam (Brawling) — Unmounted

Hurl your mass into an opponent at speed. Both combatants must be on foot. A foe
with the right weapon may respond with **Set Spear** if they see it coming from
Long distance.
- **Distance:** Close Quarters (**−5**), Normal, or Long (**+5**). Using a shield
  adds **+5**.
- **Contest:** Brawling vs. opponent's Action
- **Win:** roll damage normally (crit **+4D6**) but apply only **Brawling Damage**
  as a wound (armor protects normally). If total damage rolled **> opponent's
  SIZ**, both fall; if **> twice SIZ**, only the opponent is knocked prone.
