## Defend (Act) — Mounted, Unmounted

Forgo damage in favor of protecting yourself. A smart choice against
overwhelming odds.
- **Distance:** Any melee distance
- **Contest:** Weapon Skill **+10** vs. opponent's Action
- **Win or Tie:** take no damage, deal no damage.

## Dodge (Act) — Unmounted

Throw yourself aside to avoid damage. Your single roll is pitted against **all**
opponents' rolls and forgoes the usual multiple-opponents penalty. Usable at any
distance against melee attacks, but you must be on your feet. **Melee only — use
Zigzag for missiles.**
- **Distance:** Any melee distance
- **Contest:** Movement Rate vs. opponent's Action
- **Critical:** avoid damage, deal none, and begin next round ready to act.
- **Win:** avoid damage, deal none, but start next round on the ground (weapon
  still in hand).
- **Lose:** take all damage; start next round on the ground (weapon in hand).
- **Fumble:** take all damage, fall, start on the ground next round having
  dropped your weapon.

## Evade (Act) — Mounted, Unmounted

Disengage from melee. Like Dodge, negates and deals no damage, and also carries
you **away** from combat.
- **Distance:** Any melee distance
- **Contest:** Movement Rate (horse's, if mounted) vs. opponent's Action
- **Win:** neither take nor deal damage; no longer engaged.
- **Fumble:** fall, start next round on the ground; if mounted, fall from the
  horse and take **1D6** damage.
