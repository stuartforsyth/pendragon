Distances in melee vary according to the positions of combatants and their
weapons. Some actions require sustained physical contact, some weapons are only
effective at close quarters, and some weapons are so long they are effective
outside of normal melee distances.

Distances are expressed as one of **five qualitative categories**: Contact,
Close Quarters, Normal, Long, and Missile.

Characters may declare at which distance they wish to engage their opponent as
part of their action declaration; otherwise the Gamemaster determines it. When
combatants are within one yard's distance of each other, melee combat is
possible. To avoid combat and move past the enemy, Evasion may be necessary.
