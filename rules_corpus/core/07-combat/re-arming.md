In combat, shafts are shattered and swords are dropped on a regular basis. How
long it takes to re-arm is circumstantial and subject to the Gamemaster's
ruling, but here are some guidelines:

- A character with a **sheathed sword or dagger** at their side may draw it at
  the start of a Combat Round without penalty. Weapons in any other location
  (strapped across the back, hung from a saddle or belt, etc.) impose a **−5
  penalty** to your Combat Action that round.
- Characters **on foot** may retrieve a dropped weapon within easy reach; this
  takes a Combat Action and may be opposed (see "Pick Up", p. 141).
- **Mounted** characters who drop a weapon may not retrieve it without
  dismounting first.

Re-arming with a weapon brought forth by your squire requires the **"Call
Squire"** Action (p. 138). You may drop any weapon or shield without taking a
Combat Action — so a knight who charges with their lance on the first round may,
at the start of the second round, drop the lance, draw their sword, and still
Attack.
