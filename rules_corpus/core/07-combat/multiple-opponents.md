title: Multiple Opponents & Modifier Stacking

Up to **three unmounted** (or **two mounted**) characters may attack one
opponent, since horses take up more space. A character facing several foes must
perform the **same action** against each and applies the Multiple Opponent
modifier separately per action.

**Multiple Opponent Modifier:** a cumulative **−5 per opponent after the first**
(two foes = −5 each; three = −10). Each action is resolved separately. A
character may instead **ignore** one or more opponents — each ignored opponent
gets an unopposed action, but imposes no −5.

**Charging Multiple Opponents** (GM approval): a Mounted Charge may hit several
close foes if they are all within the horse's gallop Movement Rate, in a roughly
straight line, each making an opposed roll, and the weapon isn't broken (a broken
weapon ends the run). The Multiple Opponent modifier applies to all rolls, with
**no cap** on targets.

**Modifier order:** apply **Personal Modifiers** first (they form the base the
Multiple Opponent modifier acts on) — these are **+10 from Defend** and magical
effects. Then apply the Multiple Opponent modifier. Then apply **Situational
Modifiers** (all Combat Modifiers, and Passion modifiers), which affect only the
specific opponent being fought.
