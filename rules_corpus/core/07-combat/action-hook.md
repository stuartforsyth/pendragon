Hook uses a bill, reversed longsword, or hooked farm implement (hoe, rake,
scythe) to snag an opponent and pull them to the ground.
- **Distance:** Any melee distance except Contact
- **Contest:** Weapon Skill vs. opponent's Action
- **Win:** inflict no damage; instead make an unopposed **STR roll** — on success
  it pulls the opponent down (ending the round on the ground) or unseats a rider
  (who takes **1D6** fall damage). On a **critical**, the opponent is
  automatically pulled down.
