Riders of combat-trained horses may Trample **prone** opponents within their
Movement Rate. Even Chivalrous knights may use it in the heat of battle, at least
against armed foes. Horses dislike stepping on objects, which **cancels the
rider's Height bonus** (though the opponent still suffers a penalty).
- **Distance:** Normal
- **Contest:** Horsemanship vs. opponent's Action **−5**
- **Win:** opponent takes a wound using the horse's **normal (non-Charging)
  Damage** (see Table 10.2: Combat Horses).
