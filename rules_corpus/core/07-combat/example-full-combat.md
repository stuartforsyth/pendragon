A multi-round worked example (Sir Clydno ambushed by a gang of robbers while
mounted) demonstrating how actions and modifiers interact: Passion rolls
(Impassioned +10), Mounted Charge against multiple foes, the on-foot-vs-mounted
−5 penalty, Height Advantage, and Multiple Opponent penalties.

Not transcribed here — this is narrative illustration rather than rules. Consult
the Core Rulebook pp. 153–158 directly if you need the step-by-step walkthrough.
The mechanics it exercises are captured in [[core.combat.combat-round]],
[[core.combat.multiple-opponents]], [[core.combat.combat-modifiers]], and
[[core.combat.action-mounted-charge]].
