A **Combat Action** is anything a character chooses to do during a Combat Round.
The list below is the most common options; Gamemasters should ad-lib new ones
using these as a model.

**Action classifications:**

- **Act** — a non-Combat action (often a Characteristic or non-Combat Skill
  roll) that affects combat but does not damage the opponent.
- **Brawling** — damages/affects an opponent at Contact or Close Quarters,
  unarmed or with small/improvised weapons.
- **Combat** — damages/affects an opponent at Close Quarters or greater distance.
- **Communication** — conveys information; usually no die roll.
- **Follow-up Action** — triggered by a successful Grapple.

**Actions list:**

- *Mounted or Unmounted:* Attack, Call Squire, Claim a Prisoner, Defend, Disarm,
  Evade, Mount Up, Pick Up, Reckless Attack, Self-sacrifice, Study, Withhold
  Damage, Zigzag
- *Exclusively Unmounted:* Dodge, Don Armor, Hook, Set Spear
- *Exclusively Mounted:* Control Mount, Mounted Charge, Trample
- *Brawling:* Barehand Attack, Escape, Grapple, Slam

Each definition lists a **Distance** and a **Contest**; a **Results** subheading
appears only when outcomes differ from Table 7.1.
