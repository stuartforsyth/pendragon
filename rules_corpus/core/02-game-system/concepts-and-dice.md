## Statistics & Values

A **Statistic** quantifies a character quality and always has a numerical
**value**. Characteristics, Traits, Passions, Skills, Glory, and Armor / Parry /
Shield Protection are all Statistics. Values run on a scale of **1–20 (or more)**
and are recorded next to the Statistic (e.g. *Valorous 16, Sword 15*). A value may
rise or fall over time or be temporarily modified for a single roll.

### Trait & Passion value ranges

Trait and Passion values fall into qualitative bands:

| Band | Value | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Unsung** | 0–4 | Never known for it; does not define personality |
| **Normal** | 5–15 | Defines personality more as it nears 15 |
| **Famous** | 16–20 | Well-known for it, at least within their home county |
| **Exalted** | 20 (+x) | A critical bonus over 20; completely defines them, known everywhere |

## Dice

Pendragon uses only **d6** and **d20**. The d20 determines success or failure for
**all Statistic tests**; d6s handle damage and other randomized outcomes. Roll all
needed dice together when possible.

Notation:

- **D** — number and type of dice: the number left of the D is how many dice, the
  number right is how many sides. `5D6` = five six-sided dice.
- **1D20** — a single twenty-sided die.
- **1D3** — roll a d6 where 1–2 = 1, 3–4 = 2, 5–6 = 3.
- **+x** — a fixed number added to a result (e.g. `5D6+2` sword damage).

## Roll vs. Check (two different words)

- **Roll** = physically throw the die/dice to generate a random number.
- **Check** = place an experience check-mark in the box beside a Statistic, as a
  reminder to test it for improvement in the Winter Phase. See
  [[core.game-system.experience-checks]].

E.g. "Roll your Chaste Trait, and if you succeed, check it."

## Open vs. secret rolls

The default is to roll **in the open** (no script immunity — commit to random
results). The **exception** is Skills used for **inquiry** purposes (see Skill
Uses, p.95): the GM may roll these in secret and mislead players who fail,
withholding the check/Glory until after the encounter — and may even tell the
player to check the Skill on a failure, to preserve the illusion.

## Rounding

Values and dice outcomes round to the **nearest whole number**: a decimal of
0.1–0.4 is dropped; 0.5 or higher rounds up (3.4 → 3, 3.5 → 4).

## Quick Values (GM shortcut)

To make an opposed resolution with no Statistics at hand, roll **1D20 twice**: the
first roll *is* the Statistic's value, the second *is* the test. (Roll 8 then 12 =
"Dancing 8, and she failed.") Alternatively roll a suitable **`nD6 + modifier`** to
generate a value in a desired range (e.g. `2D6+8` for a Chaste value). Reserved for
interactions of little consequence — anything important deserves real Statistics.
