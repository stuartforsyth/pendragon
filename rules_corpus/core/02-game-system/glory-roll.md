A **Glory Roll** is a roll against the character's **Glory ÷ 1,000** (rounded to the
nearest whole number). E.g. 3,755 Glory → roll **4 or less** to succeed
(3,755 ÷ 1,000 = 3.755 → 4).

It features most often at **feasts** (see [[gm.arthurian-acts.feasting]]), but two
knights may make an **opposed** Glory Roll to settle a minor social impasse at any
time.
