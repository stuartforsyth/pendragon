Five **Characteristics** quantify a character's physical qualities and generate a
set of **Derived Characteristics**. Characteristic rolls are simple **unopposed**
resolutions with **no critical/fumble effects and no experience checks**; they may
carry a modifier (usually environmental).

## The five Characteristics

- **SIZ (Size)** — a GM may set a SIZ value on a tight passage that too-large
  knights cannot pass; an opposed SIZ roll is needed to hurl/budge any object
  larger than ¼ of your SIZ. Also the **Knockdown** value (see
  [[core.combat.knockdown]]).
- **DEX (Dexterity)** — climbing, sneaking, swimming, balancing, throwing; keeps
  you upright/horsed; sets initial weapon Skill values; some Combat Actions need a
  DEX roll.
- **STR (Strength)** — feats of raw power (break doors, lift, pull people from
  under fallen horses), often via Fixed Opposition.
- **CON (Constitution)** — resist poisons/diseases, hold breath, onerous tasks (see
  Ch.11).
- **APP (Appeal)** — non-verbal impression (bearing, voice, symmetry); sets some
  social Skill values and matters at feasts. A knight in armor with a closed helm
  has virtually **no APP** (gender not even tellable). Severe disfigurement (from
  wilderness wandering or Madness) imposes **−10 to others' Recognize**, and Glory
  does not aid the Recognize Skill unless the coat of arms is displayed.

## Derived Characteristics

| Derived value | Formula |
|---------------|---------|
| **Knockdown** | = SIZ |
| **Major Wound** | = CON |
| **Total Hit Points** | = CON + SIZ |
| **Unconscious** | = Total Hit Points ÷ 4 |
| **Healing Rate** | = CON ÷ 5 |
| **Weapon Damage** (dice) | = (STR + SIZ) ÷ 6, in d6 |
| **Brawling Damage** (flat) | = (STR + SIZ) ÷ 6, as a flat number |
| **Movement Rate** | = (STR + DEX) ÷ 2, + 5 |
