Every **Trait, Passion, and Skill** has a checkbox. Placing a **check** marks the
Statistic to make an **experience roll during the Winter Phase** (see Ch.14) to see
if the character improved. Checks are **not automatic** on any success — awarding or
denying them is one of the GM's key powers, and not all checks come from a roll.

## When a Player-knight should gain a check

- On a **critical success** (doing your best, learning is likely).
- On a **success under significant circumstances**.
- On a **fumble** (we learn from our greatest mistakes).

The GM may also grant checks for **player actions without any roll** — e.g. a player
who actually composes and sings a love song may be told to check **Compose** on GM
approval alone.

## Checking Traits & Passions without a roll

The GM may tell a player to check a **Trait or Passion** when the character has
displayed significant behavior in accordance with it — and it need not have been
witnessed by others. (A knight who lies to his lady about his fidelity might be told
to check **Deceitful**.)
