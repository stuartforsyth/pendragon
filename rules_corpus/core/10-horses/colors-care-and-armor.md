## Colors & Passive Glory

A horse's color is a social identifier and grants **Passive Glory** (once per year per
color owned; different colors stack):

- **White** — 50 (born white, rare; a grand gift only, not purchasable).
- **Gray** — 25 (+£25) · **Bay** — 15 (+£15) · **Yellow** — 5 (+£5; the minimum
  acceptable steed for a Superlative-standard knight/lady).
- **Pale** — hard to find (fairy/omen horses); **Black** — least expensive.

## Care & First Aid — Table 10.6

If a horse endures something extraordinary, takes damage, or is wounded, roll
**Horsemanship**:

| Result | Immediate | Long-term |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Critical | Energized: may move fast for **double** the normal duration | No effect |
| Success | Carries on as normal | No effect |
| Failure | Weak/sore: **−5** to all Horsemanship rolls until a farrier tends it | **−1** to next Winter Phase Horse Survival Roll |
| Fumble | Terribly weak/sore: **−10** until a farrier tends it | **−2** to next Horse Survival Roll |

A character with **Horsemanship 15+** may apply **First Aid** to wounded steeds: horse
Healing Rate = CON/5, one roll per wound (as for humans).

Horse armor is **not** issued automatically (even to household knights); horses must have
sufficient **STR** to wear it.
