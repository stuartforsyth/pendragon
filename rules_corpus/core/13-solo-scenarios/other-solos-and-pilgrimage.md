## Lost in the Woods (Table 13.3)

A knight who becomes Lost is **Out of Game** and plays this Solo in the Winter Phase.
**Once per season**, roll **Hunting** (regain the trail) and **Awareness** (spot a
settlement) — each modifies the Table 13.3 roll by **+10 crit / +5 success / −5 failure
/ −10 fumble** — then roll **1D20 on Table 13.3**. Stop on a **Return Home** result;
surviving all four seasons without one = lost another year.

Sample Table 13.3 results: low rolls = scarce food (lose 1 CON) or aimless wandering;
mid = frightening beasts/bandits (flee, −5 next season); **malicious fairy** → gain
**Fear (Fairies)** Obsession (and lose an eye / −5 Awareness, or roll on Table 14.4);
high rolls = a villager, hunter, hermit, or wild man leads you home (with a relevant
check). 20+ = reach the destination.

## Madness (Table 13.4)

A Maddened character passes to the GM, who rolls **once per year** on Table 13.4:
Madness Activity until memory is restored, the Madness is cured, or the GM brings them
back. Results range from shepherds/knights/hermits/Druids finding and healing them, to a
unicorn's breath restoring their senses, to a malicious fairy holding them. (See
[[core.traits-passions.afflictions]].)

## Pilgrimage

A religious journey to a distant holy site. Groups travel at the **slowest** pilgrim's
pace (on foot = **half** Normal Rate). Bring funds for food, lodging, and alms. Visiting
a **Royal City** (Table 13.5) or a **Great Lord** (Table 13.6) uses the same
roll-then-check-Statistics structure as the Standard Activity Solo (no roll for a
Passion you lack; both halves of a Trait pair can be checked).

After completing a pilgrimage, make an unopposed **Devotion (Deity)** roll (also checks
Devotion):

- **Critical:** choose **3 Miracles**.
- **Success:** choose **1 Miracle**.
- **Failure:** no Miracle.
- **Fumble:** no Miracle and **no pilgrimages for 1D6 years**.

**Miracles list:** +1 to a Child or Horse Survival roll; +1D3 Healing Rate for a year;
cure Disease/Poison (no longer Debilitated); cure Melancholy/Madness (if chaperoned/
bound); heal +1D3+3 HP; restore +1D2 to a Characteristic; or a story/plot miracle (GM's
call).

**Sites:** Table 13.7 (Christian — e.g. Glastonbury Abbey, Canterbury, Salisbury
Cathedral) and Table 13.8 (Pagan — e.g. Stonehenge, The Waters of Sulis, White Horses);
each lists the Traits/Skills to check (always includes **Spiritual** and **Religion**).
