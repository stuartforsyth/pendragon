Solo Scenarios **grant no direct Glory** and are optional. A player who **missed one or
more game years** may run one Standard Activity Solo per missed year.

- **Step 1 — Find activity:** roll **1D20 on Table 13.1: Household Knight's Duties** for
  the year's dominant activity, which yields a set of **check rolls**. Also attend
  **Regular Court** (always: Courtesy, Hunting, Intrigue, Recognize, Religion; one
  religious Trait; Hospitality, Honor, Love (Person); + three chosen Courtly Skills).
- **Step 2 — Roll for checks:** roll each indicated Statistic; **success → check** (no
  penalty for plain failure). **Critical = 10 Glory.** A fumbled **Trait** roll checks
  the opposing Trait; a fumbled **Passion** roll loses 1 point; Skill fumbles carry no
  penalty. Only one check per Statistic (but you can check **both** halves of a Trait
  pair).

## Table 13.1 — Household Knight's Duties (1D20)

| 1D20 | Event | Check rolls / effect |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| 1–3 | Escort | Awareness, Courtesy, Horsemanship, Charge; Temperate/Indulgent; one Weapon Skill |
| 4–9 | Garrison & Patrol | Awareness, Courtesy, First Aid, Recognize; Honest/Deceitful; one Weapon Skill |
| 10 | Illness | CON roll; fumble → Table 14.4 (Characteristic Lost); a 6 there lets you take Training & Practice **twice** in Winter Phase |
| 11–13 | Tournament | Awareness, Horsemanship, Charge, Recognize, Sword; Generous/Selfish |
| 14–16 | Unusual Event | Roll on Table 13.2 |
| 17–18 | — | Roll twice, ignoring 17+ |
| 19–20 | — | Roll thrice, ignoring 17+ |

## Table 13.2 — Unusual Events (2D6)

2 Visit royal court · 3 Visit a lord's overlord · 4 Escort far (2+ counties) · 5–6
Escort nearby · 7–8 False alarm (reroll on 13.1) · 9–10 Local tournament · 11 Visit a
lord's overlord · 12 Uprising among commoners.

Ask the GM what the **liege lord** did that year — a knight in service shares in those
events (extra experience/Glory, GM's option).
