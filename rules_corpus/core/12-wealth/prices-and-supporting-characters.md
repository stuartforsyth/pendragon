Knights avoid cash transactions (the province of merchants); when an item is **given**,
use its market value to set the Favor owed. Items under £1 are acquired more or less
automatically. **Royal mints** are the only coin source.

## Representative prices (Boy King Period)

- **War horses:** Hobby £2 · Small Charger £4 · Charger £8. **Riding:** Inferior Rouncy
  120d. · Rouncy £1 · Large Rouncy £1½ · Jennet/Courser £2. **Work:** Sumpter 120d. ·
  Mule/Donkey 60d. (see [[core.horses.types-and-stats]] for training/color surcharges).
- **Weapons:** Cheap Sword 60d. · Seax 40d. · Club 20d. · Cudgel/Quarterstaff 10d. ·
  Bow 30d. · Light Crossbow 60d. · Francisca 15d. · Javelin 10d.
- **Armorer:** Scutum 30d. · Simple Shield/Targe 10d. · Half Gambeson (horse) 120d. ·
  Full Gambeson (horse) £1 · Full Plain Caparison 90d.
- **Gear:** Travel Gear (½ horse-load) 120d. · War Gear (one pack-horse load, incl.
  travel) £1.

## Supporting characters — Squires

A squire owns only clothes, a knife, bowl, spoon, and blanket (the knight provides all
else). A knight may keep **up to three** squires at arms (£1/year each for upkeep + a
horse); the first is the **Body Squire** with authority over the others.

- **Skills:** a needed Skill value defaults to **5**, except Horsemanship, Brawling,
  Charge, Sword, and one non-weapon Skill, which start at **current age − 9** (± Table
  12.7). **Homage (Lord) begins at 15.**
- **Starting age:** roll 1D20 — **14 or less → age 14**; **15+ → that age**. Knighted or
  released as an esquire at **21**.
