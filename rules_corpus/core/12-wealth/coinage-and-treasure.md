Coins are rare until after the Conquest Period; non-lordly knights mostly barter, but
goods have money-equivalent values (a vassal knight's £10 annual income comes as food
and labor, not coin).

## Coins

- **Librum (£)** — gold; **£1 = 240d.** Mostly a **unit of account** (household knights
  rarely see gold).
- **Denarius (d.)** — silver penny; the usual cash. **1d. = £1/240.**

**£1 equivalences:** a peasant family's yearly support; a squire + two horses for a
year; a feast for 30 nobles (or 100 commoners); a scholar's annual upkeep. **1d.:** a
knight's meal; three crossbow bolts; a monk reading a letter aloud.

## Sources of treasure

Land is the most desired wealth; otherwise **ransom** is the most common income (see
[[core.wealth.ransom]]), plus **loot** (portable coin/treasure for various actions).
