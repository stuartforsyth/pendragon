## Inherited Glory

At age 14 a character inherits **one-quarter of their knightly parent's Glory** (at
that time, or at the parent's death), up to an inherited maximum of **4,000 points**.
Every starting character thus begins with reflected Glory. If both parents are
knights, use the **higher** of the two Glory values.

To find the parent's Glory if unknown: either use the **Quick Family History**
below, or roll **6D6 × 100** and add **2,000** (for inherited Glory, knighting,
marriage, and title).

## Quick Family History

Assume the parent was born 462, squired 476, knighted & married 483, and their
eldest child (the PK) born 487. The parent starts with **(2D6×100) + 2,000** Glory.
By the time the PK turns 14 (in 501), the parent earned an **additional
(3D6×100) + 500** Glory. **For each full 500 of that additional Glory, roll once on
Table 3.1: Heroic Events.** These become family lore the PK can invoke for clout.
