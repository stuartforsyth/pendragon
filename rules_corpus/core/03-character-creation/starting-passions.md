**Passions** are intense beliefs that can inspire (boost a single Skill briefly). A
Passion over **15 compels** the character to act on it. Passions are grouped into
four **courts** — **Fidelitas, Fervor, Adoratio, Civilitas** — plus **Honor**, which
stands alone. **The total of all Passions in each court is capped at 40.** PKs start
with at least one Passion in each court:

- **Fidelitas:** **Homage (Lord)** (household knights) — *mercenaries take **Fealty**
  instead*.
- **Fervor:** **Love (Family)**, perhaps a **Hate**.
- **Adoratio:** **Devotion (Deity)**.
- **Civilitas:** **Station** and **Hospitality**.
- **Cultural Passions** (e.g. **Hate (Saxons)** for all Salisbury folk in the Boy
  King period).

A knight needs **Honor of at least 5** to qualify for their station and retain
knighthood. PKs pursuing an Ideal (Chivalry, Romance) should check Chapter 6.

## Constructed method

Start with **Honor** and **Homage (Lord)** at **15**; **Love (Family), Station,
Hospitality** at **10**; **Devotion (Deity)** at **5**. Salisbury PKs also get **Hate
(Saxons) at 5**. Distribute an extra **15 points** among these or other Passions. **No
Passion above 15** by this method.

## Random method

**Honor** and **Homage (Lord)** at **2D6+8**; **Love (Family), Hospitality, Station**
at **2D6+3**; **Devotion (Deity)** at **1D6+2**; Salisbury's **Hate (Saxons)** at
**1D6+2**. Distribute **no more than 4D6+1** further points. **No Passion above 15.**

## Inherited Passions (optional)

To pass a parent's Passion to their child: take the **parent's Passion value − 15**
and add that difference (**max +5**) as a bonus to the child's starting value. Use the
parent's value at the child's creation, or at the parent's death if earlier.
