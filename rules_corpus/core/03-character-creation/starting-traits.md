**Traits** guide a character's actions and come in **pairs summing to 20**, written
as `13/7`. As one rises the other falls. A character tends to act on the higher
Trait; a value of **16+** may dictate behavior even at inopportune moments (and
grants Glory). Full rules: **Chapter 4**.

**The 13 Trait pairs:** Chaste/Lustful, Energetic/Lazy, Forgiving/Vengeful,
Generous/Selfish, Honest/Deceitful, Just/Arbitrary, Merciful/Cruel, Modest/Proud,
Prudent/Reckless, Spiritual/Worldly, Temperate/Indulgent, Trusting/Suspicious,
Valorous/Cowardly.

## Religious Virtues (underline on sheet)

- **Christian:** Chaste, Forgiving, Merciful, Modest, Spiritual, Temperate
- **Pagan:** Lustful, Energetic, Generous, Honest, Proud, Spiritual

## Constructed method

- All Traits start at **10**.
- Raise the Traits your **religion favors to 13**.
- **Valorous always begins at 15** (martial training).
- Raise **one Trait to 16**.
- Distribute **6 points** freely (no Trait above 15 by this step).

## Random method

Roll **2D6+3** for each **left-hand** Trait, top to bottom — **except Valorous,
which is 2D6+8**. When you roll a **Religious Trait** for your faith (e.g. Energetic
for Christians — *note the book's example; Energetic is a Pagan virtue*), add **+3**;
when you roll the **opposite** of one of your religious virtues, subtract **−3**.
(Random values may exceed 15.)

**Fill in opposites:** each right-hand value = **20 − left-hand**; confirm every pair
sums to exactly 20.
