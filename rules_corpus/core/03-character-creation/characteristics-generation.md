All knights generate the five Characteristics the same way regardless of gender.
(Uses/derived values: see [[core.game-system.characteristics]].)

## Constructed method

**Distribute 60 points** among SIZ/DEX/STR/CON/APP, no final value below **8** or
above **15** (before the cultural modifier). **Cymric** characters then add the
**Cultural Characteristic Modifier of +3 CON**.

## Random method — Table 3.3 (Random Cymric Characteristic Values)

Roll as shown, then apply the Cymric **+3 CON** modifier.

| Characteristic | Roll | Cultural Maximum* |
|----------------|------|-------------------|
| SIZ | 2D6+5 | 18 |
| DEX | 2D6+5 | 18 |
| STR | 2D6+5 | 18 |
| CON | 2D6+5 | 21 |
| APP | 2D6+5 | 18 |

\*Cultural Maximum = the highest value reachable over the character's lifetime
through normal improvement.

> Advice: STR+SIZ ≥ 21 gives Damage 4D6 (≥ 27 gives 5D6); CON ≥ 11 for decent Hit
> Points. Low Characteristics can be reduced further by wounds/aging until bedridden.

### SIZ equivalents (reference)

Average weight/height per SIZ (±5 lb / ±1½"): SIZ 5 ≈ 62 lb / 4'3"; 8 ≈ 95 lb /
4'11"; 10 ≈ 125 lb / 5'5"; 12 ≈ 144 lb / 5'8"; 15 ≈ 177 lb / 6'1"; 18 ≈ 218 lb /
6'6"; 21 ≈ 268 lb / 7'; 25 ≈ 354 lb / 7'8".

## Table 3.4 — Distinctive Features (by APP)

APP sets an appearance category and how many Distinctive Features (chosen by the
player, GM approval; some may be **heritable**). Positive/negative feature word-lists
(Physique, Limbs, Hair, Face, Speech) are examples only.

| APP | Appearance | Features |
|-----|-----------|----------|
| 5 or less | Infirm | At death's door |
| 6–7 | Unseemly | Three negative |
| 8–9 | Ill-favored | Two negative |
| 10–12 | Plain | One negative, one positive |
| 13–15 | Fair | Two positive |
| 16–18 | Elegant | Three positive |
| 19 or more | Surpassing | Four positive |
