## Three creation methods (pick one, don't cherry-pick)

- **Pregenerated** — copy a template from p.248, fill remaining details.
- **Constructed** — spend a fixed number of points as you choose (a character strong
  where you want, weaker elsewhere).
- **Random** — dice generate most Statistics; produces quirky, uneven characters.

## Personal information

Default assumptions: it is **508**, a **Cymric** knight of **Salisbury** (residing at
Sarum), lord is Robert, future Count of Salisbury; the PK is the designated heir of a
vassal knight. Core PKs are all **Cymric** (the most numerous, most play-advantaged
culture); other cultures/homelands live in supplements.

- **Gender** — knights may be **Sir** or **Dame** with **no mechanical difference**.
  Characteristics are generated identically regardless of gender. (See
  [[pendragon-gender-class-rule]].)
- **Name** — generally a single personal name; no surnames.

## Table 3.2 — Starting Religion (Random, roll 1D6)

Weighted toward a mostly-Christian population; GMs may adjust.

| 1D6 | Religion |
|-----|----------|
| 1–5 | Christian |
| 6 | Pagan |

Constructed method: simply **choose Christian or Pagan**.

> **No intelligence stat:** nothing quantifies intelligence/wisdom. Traits &
> Passions measure emotion, Characteristics the body, Skills specific cultural
> knowledge.
