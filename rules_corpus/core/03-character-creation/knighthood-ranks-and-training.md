## Ranks on the road to knighthood

- **Page** — boys and girls aged **7–14** learning courtly life. Most girls then
  become maids-in-waiting/wives; all boys and some girls become squires.
- **Squire** — serves a knight to learn knighthood. Those with promise, confidence,
  and connections may become knights; the rest usually stay squires past 21 and
  become **esquires** (not serving a specific knight). **All starting characters
  begin as squires on the cusp of knighthood.**
- **Knight** — most squires serve until **age 21** before knighting. Younger is
  exceptional (Lancelot at 18); a lord may hastily ennoble a young heir at **18** or,
  rarely, **15**.

## Qualifying for knighthood

A squire must be **at least 18** and have **value 10** in each of **Sword, Charge,
Brawling**, and **two non-weapon Knightly Skills** of choice (usually Horsemanship
and Courtesy). High Honor and Valorous are advisable but not required. A squire who
hasn't qualified by 21 begins play as an **esquire**. Players who wish to begin as
knights always may; the GM may allow slight Skill adjustments or an in-game
justification to qualify.

## Training & Practice (squire years)

For a first character, invest **up to 7 years** of Training & Practice (a modified
Winter Phase). **For each year of training, select one:**

- Distribute **5 points** among Skills (same limits as Personal Skill Additions —
  see [[core.character-creation.starting-skills]]).
- Add **1 point to any Trait** (max 19) **or Passion** (max 20).
- Add **1 point** to STR, SIZ, DEX, CON, or APP (this does **not** retroactively
  change beginning Skill values). No Characteristic may exceed its **Cultural
  Maximum**.

## Age & year born

Note age and year born: ask the GM the current game year and subtract your age. The
default starting age is **21**.
