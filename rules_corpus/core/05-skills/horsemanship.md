## Horsemanship (Skill)

- **Categories:** Combat, Knightly, Ladies
- **Use:** Deed, Inquiry
- **Glory:** No

Feudal society is an equine society: horses are central to a knight's existence.
**All noble men and women have this Skill** and begin learning it young, so
**riding at any pace requires no roll** under ordinary conditions.

A Horsemanship roll is called for only in **difficult circumstances**, such as:
- Jumping ditches and hedges
- Racing, chasing, or covering long distances at speed
- Horse care and tending to unusual circumstances
- Riding side-saddle at a trot, canter, or gallop
- Appraising a horse's value, outfitting it properly, and anything else
  associated with horses
- **Contests** where competitors try to outdo each other, even in ordinary
  equine affairs

As an **Inquiry** skill it also supplies knowledge: e.g. to figure out the breed
of a horse, roll Horsemanship.

**Not for training horses** — that is a separate Skill outside a knight's or
lady's knowledge.

## Fumbling a Horsemanship roll

### General (Deed) fumble — printed p. 95
Horsemanship is a **Deed** skill (e.g. riding through difficult terrain). The
general resolution outcomes apply:
- **Failure:** the character does not accomplish the deed.
- **Fumble:** the character does not accomplish the deed **and takes an
  experience check** to the Skill. The Gamemaster **may impose a negative
  modifier** to a follow-up resolution of relevant Traits, Passions, or Skills
  (e.g. a botched jump that scatters the hunt, unseats the rider, or spooks the
  horse into the next roll).

The specific bad thing that happens on a fumble is left to the GM and the
situation; falling from the mount, injuring the horse, or losing a race are
typical.

### Combat-specific fumbles
When Horsemanship is rolled inside the combat system, the dedicated fumble
entries govern the result instead of the generic Deed rule:
- **Control Mount** fumble → rider is **thrown** (see [[core.combat.mounted-utility-actions]]).
- **Emergency Dismount** fumble → **2D6** damage, drop all weapons; a falling
  horse lands on the rider (Table 7.4, see [[core.combat.mounted-combat]]).
- **Mounted Lance Charge** fumble → Table 7.2 consequences (see
  [[core.combat.action-mounted-charge]]).
