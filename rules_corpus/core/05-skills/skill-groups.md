Skills are grouped by profession/setting; the groups overlap.

- **Combat** — Battle, Brawling, Charge, Horsemanship, all Weapon Skills.
- **Courtly** — Compose, Courtesy, Dancing, Falconry, Fashion, Flirting, Gaming,
  Intrigue, Literacy, Orate, Play (Instrument), Recognize, Religion, Singing.
  **Courtly Skill values are capped at the character's APP at creation** (they may rise
  above APP later).
- **Minsterly** (church/monastic) — Courtesy, Industry, Literacy, Orate, Religion
  (Christian), Singing.
- **Knightly** — Awareness, Battle, Brawling, Charge, Courtesy, First Aid,
  Horsemanship, Hunting, Recognize, all Weapon Skills. (Knighthood needs value 10 in
  Sword, Charge, Brawling + two non-weapon Knightly Skills.)
- **Non-Knightly** (forbidden to knights) — **Chirurgery, Industry**. Using them
  **diminishes Honor** and doesn't appear on a knight's sheet.
- **Ladies** — Chirurgery, Courtesy, Dancing, Fashion, First Aid, Horsemanship,
  Industry, Play (Instrument), Recognize, Singing, Stewardship.
- **Woodcraft** — Awareness, Bow, First Aid, Folklore, Hafted Weapons, Hunting, Spear,
  Stewardship.

Combined modifiers may never exceed **±20**.
