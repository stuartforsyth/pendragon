Each entry lists **groups · use · Glory**. (Horsemanship: see
[[core.skills.horsemanship]]; Weapon Skills: Ch.8.)

- **Awareness** — *Knightly, Woodcraft · Inquiry · No.* Spot/hear/notice things.
  Unopposed to scan, or opposed vs an active hider (Hunting for people, Avoidance for
  creatures). See condition modifiers below.
- **Battle** — *Combat, Knightly · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Lead or (alone) survive
  large-scale conflict. Knights fight in a **conroi** of 5–15 led by a banneret; the
  Skill is used by bannerets/commanders and by a lone cut-off knight. Full rules in the
  GM Handbook.
- **Chirurgery** — *Ladies, Non-knightly · Deed, Inquiry · No.* Keeps the debilitated
  from deteriorating so natural healing can work (heals nothing directly).
  **Forbidden to knights.** See Ch.11.
- **Compose** — *Courtly · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Create poems, ballads, songs.
- **Courtesy** — *Courtly, Knightly, Minsterly, Ladies · Inquiry, Parlance · No.*
  Knowledge of noble culture, laws, and etiquette; the parlance Skill for nobles.
- **Dancing** — *Courtly, Ladies · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Court dances.
- **Falconry** — *Courtly · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Hunt with raptors (a half/full-day
  activity); a good showing grants later Courtesy/Flirting modifiers at court.
- **Fashion** — *Courtly, Ladies · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Style; enhances **Geniality**.
  Needs time + funds paid a month ahead; judged publicly. A made outfit keeps its bonus
  **one year**, then decays one step/year. Dressing above your station risks a **Proud
  check** and reprimand.
- **First Aid** — *Knightly, Ladies, Woodcraft · Deed, Inquiry · Glory for saving a
  life* (10 knight / 25 banneret or famous / 50 baron or Round Table / 75 count-duke-
  petty king / 100 king or very famous RT / 250 High King). Immediate wound care; see
  Ch.11.
- **Flirting** — *Courtly · Deed, Inquiry, Parlance · No.* Romantic banter.
- **Folklore** — *Woodcraft · Inquiry, Parlance · No.* Commoner lore/customs; the
  parlance Skill for commoners.
- **Gaming** — *Courtly · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Play dice, chess, and other games well.
- **Hunting** — *Knightly, Woodcraft · Deed, Inquiry · No.* Track, pursue, and kill
  game; a leader may roll for the group.
- **Industry** — *Ladies, Minsterly, Non-knightly · Deed, Inquiry · No.* Craft cloth,
  clothing, textiles, and vestments (Table 5.3). **Knights lose 3 Honor each time they
  engage in it.**
- **Intrigue** — *Courtly · Inquiry, Parlance · Yes.* Learn court secrets and plots;
  subtle deceptive parlance.
- **Literacy** — *Courtly, Minsterly · Inquiry, Parlance · No.* Read and write a
  Language (starting value 0).
- **Orate** — *Courtly, Minsterly · Inquiry, Parlance · Yes.* Public speaking; sway
  crowds.
- **Play (Instrument)** — *Courtly, Ladies · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Musical performance.
- **Recognize** — *Courtly, Knightly, Ladies · Inquiry · No.* Identify knights, coats
  of arms, and families.
- **Religion** — *Courtly, Minsterly · Inquiry, Parlance · No.* Knowledge of one's
  faith; the parlance Skill for clergy.
- **Singing** — *Courtly, Minsterly, Ladies · Deed, Inquiry · Yes.* Vocal performance;
  hymns at mass.
- **Stewardship** — *Ladies, Woodcraft · Inquiry · No.* Land management (for
  land-holding knights). **+1 per Literacy 1–5, +2 per 6–10, etc.** Governs dispensing
  low justice on a manor.

## Awareness condition modifiers

Applied to Awareness (combined ≤ ±20):

- **Atmosphere** (fog/rain/smoke/snow): Light −5, Moderate −10, Heavy −15
- **Background noise:** Din −5, Clamor −10, Cacophony −15
- **Concealment:** Light −5, Moderate −10, Heavy −15
- **Darkness:** Dim (half-moon) −5, Shadowy (quarter) −10, Deep (new moon) −15,
  Complete −20
- **Light source (to spot in dark):** Torch +5, Campfire +10, Large campfire +15,
  Bonfire +20
- **Volume:** raised voices +5, shouting +10, trumpets +15 · **Taste:** additives −5

**Fire's light** removes penalties within its radius and reduces darkness one step out
to double the radius: Bonfire 25 ft, Large campfire 15 ft, Campfire 10 ft, Torch 6 ft,
Candle 3 ft.
