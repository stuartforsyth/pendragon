Generates a random heraldic device. Each knight's coat of arms is unique (the GM acts as
the College of Heralds).

## Procedure

1. **Step 1 — Shield Design:** roll **1D20 on Table B.1** for the composition (a plain
   Charge, an Ordinary, a Variation, or a Division), following its instructions (some
   point to Sub-tables B.1.1 Variations / B.1.2 Divisions).
2. **Step 2 — Tinctures:** roll the **Field Tincture** on Table B.2, then pick the Metal-
   or Color-Charge column per the **Rule of Tincture**. (Divisions: roll Field Tincture
   twice, ignoring the Rule; use 1D2 to pick a charge column.)
3. **Step 3 — Charges:** roll on **Table B.3: Charges** (with Sub-table B.3.1 Charge
   Types) for the specific charge(s). For quadrupeds except lions, roll 1D6 (+1 for
   herbivores) on column 1 for the animation/pose.

### The Rule of Tincture

A **metal** charge must sit on a **color** field, and vice-versa. **Furs** may take
either (roll 1D2 for the column).

## Table B.1 — Shield Design (1D20)

| 1D20 | Design |
|------|--------|
| 1–6 | Charge (Step 3) |
| 7 | Minor Charge (group of 2D6, col.5 of B.3) |
| 8–9 | Charge (single) + Minor Charge |
| 10 | Peripheral Ordinary |
| 11 | Peripheral Ordinary + Charge on the Field |
| 12 | Peripheral Ordinary + Charge on the Ordinary |
| 13 | Ordinary (B.3 col.6) |
| 14 | Ordinary + Charge on the Field |
| 15 | Ordinary + Charge on the Ordinary |
| 16–18 | Variation (Sub-table B.1.1; a reroll = solid Variation) |
| 19–20 | Division (Sub-table B.1.2, then roll again; a reroll = solid, no charges) |

## Table B.2 — Tinctures (1D20)

| 1D20 | Field | Metal Charge | Color Charge |
|------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| 1–5 | Argent (silver) | Argent | Azure |
| 6–7 | Or (gold) | Argent | Vert |
| 8–9 | Or (gold) | Or | Gules |
| 10 | Purpure (purple) | Or | Gules |
| 11–13 | Azure (blue) | Or | Gules |
| 14–16 | Gules (red) | Or | Sable |
| 17 | Vert (green) | Or | Purpure |
| 18–19 | Sable (black) | Proper | Proper |
| 20 | Fur | Proper | Proper |

**Fur:** 1D6 → 1–4 Ermine, 5–6 Vair. **Proper:** painted in natural colors (for
Ordinaries/geometric charges read as Or for metal, Gules for color).

## Table B.3 — Charges

**Table B.3** provides the specific charges across six columns (three **Animate**, two
**Inanimate**, one **Ordinary**), rolled 1D20 × 1D6, with **Sub-table B.3.1: Charge
Types** classifying them (Peripherals, etc.) — e.g. lions and other beasts, crosses, and
geometric ordinaries. (See pp.244–246 for the full charge lists.)
