In the Pendragon RPG, shields do not have an ongoing pool of structural "Hit Points." Instead, their effectiveness and durability are governed by specific combat rules:

### **1. Standard Shield Protection**

- **Protection Value:** Each shield type has a flat Armor Protection value (generally **6** for Large or Medium shields, and **4** for Small shields).
- **Triggering Shield Protection:** A shield only absorbs damage if you score a **Partial Success** in an opposed combat resolution. If you fail your combat roll or if your opponent wins cleanly, the blow completely bypasses your shield and it provides no protection.
- **Defending Your Mount:** If you are mounted and an opponent intentionally attacks your horse, scoring a partial success allows you to apply your shield's protection value to protect your mount.

### **2. Destroying or Damaging Shields**

Standard high-quality shields (like Kite, Round, or Scutum shields) do not take wear-and-tear damage from normal weapon attacks. However, there are specific situations where a shield can be damaged, degraded, or permanently broken:

- **Axe Attacks (The Anti-Shield Weapon):** Battle Axes, Great Axes, Franciscas, and later Pollaxes are specifically designed to hack through shields. When you are hit by an axe but are able to apply your shield's protection:
    - The shield does not block its full value; instead, it **only blocks 1D6 points of damage** (up to its maximum protection value).
    - If you roll a **1 on that 1D6 block roll**, the shield immediately **breaks and is rendered useless**.
- **Simple Shields (Poor Quality):** If you are carrying a cheap, poorly made **Simple Shield** (typically used by poor infantry) and use its protection value against an opponent's **Critical Success**, the shield **shatters instantly** and is destroyed.
- **Poor Knight Standard of Living:** If a knight falls to a Poor Standard of Living, their neglected gear suffers. All of their armor (including their shield) has its protection value **permanently reduced by 1 point**.
- **Ritual Disgrace:** If a knight's Honor falls to zero, they are stripped of their rank. In a public ceremony of disgrace, their shield is **ceremonially smashed**, their sword broken, and their spurs cut off.
