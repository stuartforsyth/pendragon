**Fine amor**, or Romance, is the **chaste adulation of a lover to obtain Inspiration** — originally codified by Queen Guenever. It begins when a knight becomes infatuated with a lady or courtier, consumed by a **chaste** desire to win their favor and inspired to great deeds. The knight must think of them constantly, seek their attention, and **jealously protect their honor**.

==To gain the **Passive Glory** for the romance, the knight must have an **Adoration (Person)** Passion and fulfil the other requirements of the **Romantic Knight** ideal.==

## Increasing the Beloved's opinion

A knight raises his standing with the Beloved by **giving gifts**, **doing favors**, or **succeeding at tasks they have requested**. The Beloved may **demand a task as proof of affection**.

## Glory in the Beloved's name

**The Beloved may earn Glory for deeds done in their name.**

- ==If a knight defeats an enemy — in mortal combat **or at a tournament** — and **sends them to the Beloved** with word that they were overcome in the Beloved's name, **the Beloved earns the same Combat Glory award** as if they had defeated the enemy personally.==
- The knight may also **donate other Glory awards** earned while inspired by the Adoration (Person) Passion:
  - **Donating 10 Glory** earns a **check to Adoration**.
  - **Donating 100 Glory** automatically **raises Adoration by one point**.
  - **No more than one Adoration point per year** may be gained this way.
  - Donations must be made **at the time of the award** and come from a **single award** — they **may not be banked or combined**.

## Consummation

**Fine amor is a spiritual practice.** ==Physical consummation **annihilates the Amor**.== The **Adoration** Passion is **removed and replaced by Love at half its value** — **Love (Spouse)** if the couple marry, **Love (Person)** if not.

The Lover and Beloved may go on practising Romance, but the reward is diminished: **the knight may no longer grant his own Glory awards to his lover**, though he **may still capture knights in their name**, earning them Glory that way.

> **Period note.** From the **Romance Period** onward, fine amor is codified into **Courtly Love** and the process of adoration and seduction becomes a ritualized social dance — a more detailed method of wooing covered in other Pendragon supplements.
