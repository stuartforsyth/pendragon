How much Glory to award is always the GM's call; these are the book's suggestions. Adapt them to the Glory progression you want, but stick to them for a first campaign.

## Combat Glory — Table A.1

Awarded for **defeating** an opponent (surrendered, killed, disabled, or driven from the fight), based on the opponent's **highest modified Combat Skill used in the fight**.

| Opponent's modified weapon value | Glory |
|---|---:|
| 1–5 | 1 |
| 6–10 | 10 |
| 11–15 | 25 |
| 16–20 | 50 |
| 20 (+1)–20 (+5) | 100 |
| 20 (+6)–20 (+10) | 200 |
| 20 (+11)–20 (+20) | 400 |

Use the **highest** modified value that applied at any point: a knight with Sword 15 who becomes Impassioned counts as Sword 20, moving the award from 25 to 100. A **penalty** suffered for only part of the fight does **not** reduce the award.

**Combat "for love"** — ⅒ the normal Combat Glory. A fight is "for love" if **both sides use rebated weapons**, it is **to first knockdown or first blood**, it is **for honor**, or the prize is **worth £3 or less**.

**Against beasties and fairies** — the award is **divided among all knights** who defeated the creature:

| Creature | Glory | | Creature | Glory |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Dragon | 1,000 | | Cockatrice | 100 |
| Giant (large) | 250 | | Hag | 75 |
| Lion | 250 | | Ogre | 75 |
| Unicorn | 250 | | Bear | 50 |
| Barguest | 100 | | Wolf | 10 |
| | | | Vicious rat | 0 |

**Battles** award Glory by size, victory/defeat, and command, *plus* Combat Glory for the individual foes fought — see [[gm.battle.after-the-battle]].

## Skill use

Skills grant Glory only when used in **significant, public** venues — **a success without witnesses does not count**. Some Skills award only in specific circumstances (First Aid that brings a character back to life or saves a ranking noble).

**Glory = the character's APP.** **Doubled** on a critical, or when performing before a **king or queen**.

## Successful adventure

Awarded for completing a task, and stacking with Glory for enemies defeated. If several characters take part, **each** receives the full award.

- **Simple adventure, easily achieved:** 100
- **Regular adventure:** 200–300
- **Difficult adventure:** 300–500

## Personal valor

- **Winning combat against knights at 4:1 odds or greater:** double the normal award
- **Volunteering for a suicide mission:** +250
- **Dying performing a heroic action:** +1,000

## Afflictions

Involuntary but temporary emotional states that significantly impair the character:

- **Madness:** 100 · **Melancholy:** 25 · **Miserable:** 10
