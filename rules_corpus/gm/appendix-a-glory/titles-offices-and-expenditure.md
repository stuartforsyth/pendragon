## Honors and titles

- **Knighthood:** 1,000 — **plus** a separate award equal to **1% of the Glory of the lord who knights them**. Being knighted by Arthur is worth far more than by a minor lord.
- **Knight of the Round Table:** 1,000
- **Joining another chivalric organization:** 200

**Titles** award **once per title**, and multiple titles accumulate. Least to greatest:

| Title | Glory | | Title | Glory |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Household Knight | 50 | | Count | 1,000 |
| Vassal Knight | 250 | | Duke | 1,000 |
| Banneret | 500 | | Petty King | 1,000 |
| Baron | 1,000 | | Minor King | 1,000 |
| | | | King of Malahaut / Logres / High King | 1,000 |

**Offices** award **once per liege lord**, scaled to that lord's status, and **once per level of rank** — a knight who becomes steward to a baron gains 100 and gains nothing further for later being made that baron's marshall.

- **Officer to a baron:** 100 · **to a count or duke:** 200 · **to the Pendragon:** 400
- **Privy Councilor:** +100

## Sharing wealth

- **Money to your liege lord** to offset his responsibilities: **10 Glory per £1**, max
  100. Checks **Homage, Generous, Worldly**.
- **Public works** (city walls, baths, market): **5 Glory per £1**, max 100. Checks **Generous** and **Station**.
- **Ransoming a non-family member:** **10 Glory per £1**, max 100. Checks **Generous** and **Just** — and ==the ransomed person gains a **Loyalty** Passion toward the donor equal to the libra value of the ransom paid.==

## Extravagant spending

Totalled across the year in the **Winter Phase**, then converted: **10 Glory per £1 up to 100 Glory**, and **1 Glory per £1** thereafter.

The display must be massive, outrageous, and bring **no return or benefit** beyond Glory, a **Generous** check, and one other appropriate Trait. Naming the specific extravagance is required — "I spend the money" is not enough.

| Spend | Glory | Example (and the Trait checked) |
|---|---:|---|
| £10 | 100 | A grand feast for 300 nobles or 1,000 commoners (**Generous**); a feast for 30 nobles of rare imported meats (**Worldly**); *Gala Bohort!* — a "Kill the Beast" contest with caged wild animals fought with daggers (**Cruel**) |
| £25 | 115 | *Trojan Games!* — ancient sports, knightly (horse race, eating contest, "Odysseus' Bow") and common (javelin, wrestling, cart races) (**Proud**) |
| £50 | 140 | A feast for 30 nobles consisting **only of spices** (**Indulgent**); *Money Wagon!* — devils clearing the way, trumpeters, angels flinging ha'pence (**Arbitrary**) |
| £100 | 190 | *Field of Riches!* — bears harnessed to a plough while women in "Pagan costume" sow droplets of silver (**Reckless**) |

## Building and endowing houses of worship

**Building:** Glory = **10× the structure's cost**, max 500. Checks **Spiritual** and the appropriate **Religion** Skill.

- Small church £25 (250) · Small abbey £52 (500) · Nunnery £23 (230) · Pagan temple £28 (280) · Pagan shrine £25 (250)

**Endowment** (permanently donating land or wealth to a foundation): Glory = **100× the value of the land**, max 500. Checks **Generous**, **Spiritual**, and the **Religion** Skill. The donor also earns a **Favor** from the head of the institution worth the construction and land combined.

- Small church £3 (300) · Small abbey £7 (500) · Nunnery £3 (300) · Pagan temple £6 (500) · Pagan shrine £3 (300)

## Special circumstances

- **Knighting a squire of greater social rank:** 100 · **heir to a king:** 250
- **Refusing rank** — declining an offer of land or title from a grateful monarch: **250 Glory** immediately. The rebuffed party's reaction is the GM's to decide.
- **Marriage:** Glory equal to **¼ of the spouse's Glory**, up to 1,000.

## Tournaments

- **Sponsoring:** Glory = the cost × the **Extravagant Spending** bonus.
- **Attending, participating, winning:** everyone gains Glory; amounts vary by the size of the tournament (see *Tournaments*, GM Book p. 51).
- **The Melee** is almost always fought *for love* → **⅒ normal Combat Glory**. For greater stakes (the loser's horse, large Favors, ransom) but still rebated weapons → **½ normal**. Mordred's "Blood Tournaments", with unrebated weapons, pay **full Combat Glory**.
- **Jousting:** Glory is set by the prize's value and figured **per round, not per pass**. Prize ≤ £3 → as a regular joust. Prize > £3 → **half the opponent's Charge Skill** per round.

## Witnessing and hosting special events

The **host** of the event receives **five times** the witness's award.

- **£2 baptism of an heir:** 10 (20 for godparents)
- **£1 baptism of another child:** 5 (10 for godparents)
