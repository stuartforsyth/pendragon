**Passive Glory** accrues annually in the Winter Phase for what a character *is* and *maintains*, rather than for what they did.

## Standard of Living — Table A.2

A landed knight's Standard of Living is a fraction of total income (**10% of landholdings, plus £5 if married or £4 if unmarried**). It covers personal maintenance only; a household is funded separately.

Living **above** the minimum required for Ordinary is **Conspicuous Consumption**: **10 Glory per additional £1** spent over the annual minimum of **£6** (for a knight with a squire and family). Living **below** one's normal standard carries penalties — see "Standard of Living", Core Rulebook pp. 227–228.

| Title | Typical landholding income | Upkeep | Standard of Living | Passive Glory |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| Vassal Knight | £10 (£7–£30) | £6 | Ordinary | 0 |
| Estate Holder | £50 (£30–£100) | £10 | Rich | 40 |
| Banneret Knight | £100 (£60–£120) | £15 | Superlative | 90 |
| Minor Baron\* | £150 (£100–£200) | £20 | Superlative | 100 |
| Baron | £300 (£200–£400) | £35 | Superlative | 120 |
| Rich Baron\* | £600 (£400–£800) | £65 | Superlative | 150 |
| Count, Duke (Great Baron\*) | £1,000 (£800–£1,600) | £105 | Superlative | 200 |
| King of a major kingdom† | £3,000 (£1,000–£5,000) | £305 | Superlative | 400 |

\* Not official titles — used to quantify a baron's wealth and power. † Cornwall, Malahaut, Lothian. Britain's many minor and petty kings are barely equal to barons in Logres.

## Traits and Passions

- **Famous** (16–20) Traits and Passions: **15 Glory each** per year.
- **Exalted** (20+x): **25 Glory each** per year.
- **Cap: 100 points** of Passive Glory per year from Traits, and **100** from Passions, counted separately.
- ==**Directed Traits and Obsessions generate no Passive Glory.**==

## Ideals

Some Ideals grant Passive Glory when fulfilled, as stated in their own descriptions — see [[core.aspirations.ideals]].

## Glory for Ladies

Ladies receive **the same awards as knights**, plus these:

- **Child born:** 100
- **Eldest child is knighted:** 500 · **any other child knighted:** 100
- **Any son or daughter takes a Title:** ⅒ the Glory of that title's award
- **First son to join the clergy:** 100
- **Eldest son is married:** 250
