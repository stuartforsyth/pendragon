## Disobeying orders

Refusing a conroi leader's orders loses **1 Station and 1 Homage** (plus possible
Proud/Reckless/Cowardly checks and a Suspicious/Hate from the leader), and renders the PK
**Alone on the Field** (deserters don't form their own conroi).

## Battle Turn procedure

1. **Set Encounter** (GM's default). Battle **Events** keyed to the turn apply to PKs on
   the field. The commander rolls Battle for a **Mounted Charge Opportunity** (Table 6.3),
   except turn 1.
2. **Intensity roll** (GM, unopposed vs Intensity): success = Encounter stands (**crit
   doubles foes**, **fumble = an Opportunity**); failure = PKs may reroll.
3. **Battle roll** (each PK, if they want to change): success = choose an Encounter;
   **critical = choose an Opportunity**.
4. **Select Encounter** (commander); if below Minimum Morale → **Retire to the Rear**.
5. **Reduce Morale** by the Encounter's amount.
6. **Select Posture** & invoke Passions.
7. **Fight the Encounter** (rounds per Posture).
8. **Squire Skill roll** (optional).

### Table 6.3 — Mounted Charge Opportunity

| Battle roll | Engaged last round | Unengaged last round |
|-------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Critical | May charge | May charge (**+5 Charge**) |
| Success | May not charge | May charge |
| Failure | May not charge | May not charge |
| Fumble | Enemy knights charge you! | Enemy knights charge you! |

**First Charge:** unless the Army Commander failed, **turn 1 is always a mounted lance
charge** — all pick Mounted Charge and only Reckless or Valorous Posture.

## Postures (rounds fought + Morale effect)

- **Reckless** — 3 rounds; check Reckless; **+1D3 Morale**; must Reckless-Attack round 1
  (unless charging).
- **Valorous** — 2 rounds; check Valorous.
- **Prudent** — 1 round; check Prudent; **−1D3 Morale**.
- **Cowardly** — don't engage; immediately Retire to the Rear; **−1 Honor** and **−1D6
  Morale** if above half HP.

## Fighting the Encounter

Normal melee; charging knights take Mounted Charge as their first Action. You may not aid
an ally without a successful **Evade** first. Fight your Posture's rounds (min 1); if you
drop your foe early, take a fresh one. Defeating all available foes ends the turn; a PK
still engaged at turn's end may fight one more round on a successful **Energetic +
Valorous**. **Passions** may be used once per battle (effect lasts one Battle Turn).
After the Encounter, gain each defeated foe's **Glory**, and raise Morale by the total
**KV** of foes defeated.

**Missile foes:** the commander rolls Battle to see rounds of barrage endured before
contact — **crit immediate / success 1 / failure 2 / fumble 3**. Each round, 1D6: **1–4
hits the mount, 5–6 the rider**. Shields impose their Missile Penalty (no Zigzag in a
conroi).

**Squire Skill roll** (unopposed, optional): success = squire helps (replace a lance ×2,
give a horse, escort a prisoner, bear off an unconscious knight); **fumble = squire
missing until the battle ends**. **Capture:** a knight may take an Unconscious foe
prisoner (or use Brawling / withheld blows); exposing a surrendered captive to danger =
**−1 Honor**.
