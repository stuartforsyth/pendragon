Battle ends after the maximum Battle Turns (Table 6.1 or the scenario). Major battles are
usually decided by the campaign narrative; **Clash/Skirmish** outcomes are resolved by
this system: **determine victory → Glory → ransom/plunder**.

## Victory or defeat (Knight Value)

A **Knight Value (KV)** = one armored, horsed knight. Tally each side's **losses**
(killed or captured) in KV.

### Table 6.4 — Boy King Period Knight Values

| Troop | KV |
|-------|-----|
| Peasant | 1/16 |
| Footsoldier/Spearman/Archer | 1/8 |
| Armored Footsoldier | 1/4 |
| Hobilar/Squire/Pricker | 1/4 |
| Mounted Sergeant | 1/2 |
| Poor Knight | 3/4 |
| Knight | 1 |

If the conroi includes GM knights, the commander rolls **Table 6.5: Conroi Losses**:
crit = no losses; success = 10% lost (10% captured); failure = 20% (10% killed, 10%
captured); fumble = 50% (30% killed, 20% captured).

**Outcome** = (enemy KV losses ÷ friendly KV losses), then **×2** if you greatly
outnumbered the foe (2:1+) or **×½** if greatly outnumbered:

- **≥4** Decisive Victory · **2–3.9** Victory · **0.5–1.9** Indecisive · **0.25–0.4**
  Defeat · **<0.25** Decisive Defeat.

## Glory

Every PK who fought ≥1 Encounter gets **Participation Glory** (Table 6.6), **× outcome
multiplier** (Table 6.7), **× leader multiplier** (Table 6.8, if commanding a battle
larger than Skirmish) — add the multipliers together before applying. This is **on top
of** per-foe Encounter Glory.

### Table 6.6 — Participation Glory

| Size | Base | Tournament (rebated) |
|------|------|----------------------|
| Fight / Skirmish | 5 | 0 |
| Clash | 10 | 1 |
| Small Battle | 20 | 2 |
| Medium Battle | 30 | 3 |
| Large Battle | 45 | 5 |
| Huge Battle | 60 | 6 |

### Table 6.7 — Outcome multiplier

Decisive Victory ×2 · Victory ×1½ · Indecisive ×1 · Defeat ×¾ · Decisive Defeat ×½.

### Table 6.8 — Leader multiplier

Conroi Commander ×2 · Bataille Commander ×3 · Army Commander ×5. (E.g. a conroi commander
on a Victory: ×(2+1½) = **×3½** base Glory.)

Finally, resolve **ransom and plunder** from captured foes (see [[core.wealth.ransom]]).
