## Battlefield positions

**Engaged/On the Field** (choosing Encounters and fighting) · **Retire to the Rear** (the
action of moving off the field) · **Out of the Battle** (behind the lines recovering) ·
**Left the Field** (no longer in the battle).

## Retiring to the Rear

The conroi **must** retire when Morale hits **0** or is too low for the chosen Encounter
(no combat, no Glory). Otherwise the commander decides (possible Prudent/Cowardly check);
if called mid-combat, each engaged PK must **Evade** or finish their fight first.

Retiring takes the conroi out for **≥1 Battle Turn** and restores **+1D6 Morale**.
Attendants may treat wounds (roll a random **First Aid 14+1D6** per knight); lost horses
may be replaced (Squire Skill or Horsemanship). The commander may stay disengaged,
gaining **+1D6 Morale/turn** up to the starting value — then must return next turn or
declare the conroi has **Left the Field**.

## Alone on the Field

Caused by deserting, Passion-Madness, or being unhorsed and failing the Squire roll for a
remount. The PK becomes their own commander, rolling Battle at **−10**, and **still
deducts from the conroi's Morale** (faster drain). A **maddened** knight uses a personal
Morale of **1D6 + the driving Passion's value** (at 0, they vanish Out of Game). Afterward
they may continue alone (mandatory if Mad), **seek their conroi** (unmodified Recognize),
**rally a new conroi** (Glory Roll → Morale 2D6+3, or 2D6+6 on a crit; but Battle **−5**
for the rest of the battle), or Retire to the Rear (a Valorous 16+ knight checks Cowardly
unless Debilitated).

## Surrender

GM characters at **half HP or less** roll unopposed **Valorous** (−5 if on foot vs a
mounted PK):

- **Critical:** fight on until dead/unconscious.
- **Success:** fight one more round, then roll again.
- **Failure:** attempt to flee.
- **Fumble:** immediate surrender.

**Fleeing:** both mounted → opposed **Horsemanship**; both on foot → opposed **DEX**;
mounted fleeing a footman → escapes; footman fleeing a rider → caught unless allowed. PKs
choose how long to fight; a foe caught fleeing may surrender or fight on (later surrender
may require the opponent to pass a **Merciful** roll).
