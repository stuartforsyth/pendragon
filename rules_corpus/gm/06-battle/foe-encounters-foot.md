Each Foe Encounter lists **Morale Loss** (subtracted from the conroi's Morale when taken), **Morale Minimum** (the conroi may not engage below it), and **Knight Value** for the post-battle KV tally. Numbers given are **per Player-knight**.

## Peasants — Morale Loss 1D3 · Minimum 5 · KV 1/16

Levied farmers with agricultural implements. ==No Glory in fighting them, only bloodshed.==

- **Five peasants per Player-knight.**
- **If charged by knights:** roll **Cowardly** — on a *success* they flee and are cut down by the horsemen.
- Even if they stand, **only half attack per round** (three per knight), imposing the usual Multiple Opponents penalty. A peasant **automatically flees** at the end of any round in which he takes damage.
- At the end of each round, one **collective Valorous** roll per group: **failure** = half the group flees (plus the wounded); **fumble** = the whole group flees.

## Spearmen — Morale Loss 1D6 · Minimum 8 · KV 1/8

The lowest of the proper fighting men. In a hedge of spear points they are dangerous to knights *and* horses.

- **Three spearmen per knight.** They gang up, imposing the Multiple Opponents penalty.
- **Set Spear** against a Mounted Charge requires a **Valorous** roll — ==**every** spearman in the group must succeed== for the group to use it.
- They always wield spears **two-handed** (the damage already includes the +2D6), and therefore **do not suffer the −5 height penalty** against mounted opponents.
- **Defend** (backing away slowly) once more than **one-third** are seriously injured or killed; **flee at two-thirds**.

## Foot Soldiers — Morale Loss 1D6 · Minimum 7 · KV 1/8

Mercenaries or feudal garrison in light armor with shields — tougher than peasants, but reluctant to face a lance charge.

- **Two foot soldiers per knight.** One roll sets their collective armament.
- **If charged by mounted knights:** a single **collective Valorous** roll — *any success* and all stand; **failure** = half flee; **fumble** = all flee. They also all flee once **half** their number are seriously injured or killed.
- Apply the **Height modifier** when they fight mounted knights, and the long-weapon bonus for knights with spears or lances.

## Archers — Morale Loss 1D6 · Minimum 5 · KV 1/8

*"Cowardly scum, shooting at good knights!"*

- **Three archers per Player-knight.** One roll sets their collective armament.
- They **loose as soon as the knights enter long range** — use **Closing Distance** (Core Rulebook p. 152) for how many rounds they get, assuming a **canter** if the knights are charging, otherwise a **trot**. Against mounted knights roll **1D6: 1–4 hits the horse, 5–6 the rider**.
- If the conroi reaching them has **more mounted than dismounted knights**, each archer must make a **Valorous** roll or flee. The whole unit flees once **one-third** are seriously injured or killed.

## Elite Knights on Foot — Morale Loss 1D6+2 · Minimum 12 · KV 1/2

Dismounted by mishap or by choice, and every bit as deadly. The Elite are a lord's bodyguard in fine harness. **Both use the same statblock.**

- **1½ times as many as there are Player-knights** (round up).
- They **double up** on the Player-knights where possible, targeting those with **the most Glory and the best-looking equipment and horses** first; the rest fight one-on-one.
- Their **spears negate the −5 height penalty** against a mounted opponent. Against foes on foot they prefer **swords**.
- Roll ransom **individually** for each knight captured.
