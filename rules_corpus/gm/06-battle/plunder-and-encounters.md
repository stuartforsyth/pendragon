## Plunder

Player-knights may take **personal plunder** from captured foes and from raids on the enemy baggage train, as detailed in the relevant Encounters; those ransoms already account for the lord's share, the value of kit (armor, weapons, tack), and so on.

**Every participant** also receives a share of the overall loot **if the outcome is a Victory or Decisive Victory**. Unless the scenario says otherwise, the base value is set by the opposing army.

### Table 6.9 — Basic Plunder

| Opponents | Basic plunder | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Feudal army | £2 | The enemy is the equivalent of the Player-knights |
| Inferior feudal army | £1½ | Distant lands are less well-equipped and mounted |
| Saxons | £1 | Good armor, but the lack of horses affects value |
| Cymric hill tribes | £½ | Inferior arms and horses |
| Pict hill tribes, Irish warriors | £¼ | Poor people, poor armor, virtually no horses |

**On a Decisive Victory, add +£2D3** to the value of the plunder.

### Table 6.10 — Plunder Modifiers

| Circumstance | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Greatly outnumbered the enemy (2:1 or more) | **×½** |
| Own force greatly outnumbered (1:2 or worse) | **×2** |
| Conroi Commander | **+⅓** |
| Division Leader | **×3 × Battle Size Multiplier** |
| Army Leader | **×10 × Battle Size Multiplier** |

**Battle Size Multiplier:** Small ×1 · Medium ×2 · Large ×3 · Huge ×4.

## Chance of Countercharge

Opponents armed with lances — **mounted knights, mounted sergeants, Northern prickers** — **always countercharge** the Player-knights if encountered on the **first Battle Turn**.

On **subsequent turns**, roll **1D6**:

- **1–2:** Yes — they still have lances and the room to countercharge.
- **3–6:** No — their lances are shattered, or they saw the danger too late.

## Choosing Encounters

The Foe Encounters and Opportunities in this chapter are a menu, not a fixed list. Choose from them as described under "Encounters" (GM Book pp. 122–123), or use them as models for your own. **Foes** may recur; **Opportunities** come once and are reachable only on a **critical Battle roll**.
