> **Note a discrepancy:** the Knight Values printed in these Foe Encounters (Hobilar **1/2**, Mounted Sergeant **3/4**) differ from **Table 6.4** in *After the Battle*, which gives Hobilar/Squire/Pricker **1/4** and Mounted Sergeant **1/2**. Pick one and use it consistently for the whole KV tally.

## Hobilars — Morale Loss 1D6+3 · Minimum 10 · KV 1/2 (cf. Table 6.4: 1/4)

Lightly-armed scouts and pursuers on swift "hobby" horses. They want no part of a conroi of knights, and offer **little Glory and no ransom**.

- **Two hobilars per Player-knight.**
- As the knights close, they **automatically attempt Evade**; their hobby horses' **Movement Rate is 18**. A knight chasing both counts as **fighting multiple opponents (−5 per action)**; concentrate on one and ==the other automatically flees.==
- If caught, a hobilar fights **at least one Combat Round**, then tests **Valorous** — failure means another Evade attempt next round, success means another round of combat.

## Mounted Sergeants — Morale Loss 1D6+2 · Minimum 12 · KV 3/4 (cf. Table 6.4: 1/2)

Non-noble warriors trained to fight from horseback. Also use this block for **esquires, poor mercenary knights without squires**, and other better-off cavalry.

- **As many sergeants as there are Player-knights.**
- They ride **small chargers**, armed and equipped like knights but not as well.
- Trained in mounted tactics, so they **may countercharge**.
- Usually **not worth a ransom** — but determine it individually, since some are esquires or poor mercenary knights.

## Elite Mounted Knights — Morale Loss 2D6 · Minimum 12 · KV 1

**Both use the same statblock.** The Elite ride **fine stallions worth £5 more** than normal chargers, and their captured horses may have received training.

- **As many as there are Player-knights.**
- They **may countercharge**. Except on a charge they prefer **swords**, unless facing long weapons.
