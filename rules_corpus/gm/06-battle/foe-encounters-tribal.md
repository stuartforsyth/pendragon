## Pictish Clansmen — Morale Loss 1D6+1 · Minimum 8 · KV 1/8

Painted men from beyond the Wall, common in the army of Lothian and other Northern kingdoms. No armor but their tattoos and a targe on the wrist; no weapon but a spear, always two-handed. ==They care nothing for ransoms and offer none — a knight's head on a pike is reward enough.==

- **Two Pictish Clansmen per Player-knight.**
- Spears are **two-handed** (the damage already reflects the bonus). They may use **Set Spear** against a Mounted Charge ==**without needing a roll**== — they are trained in the tactic as a matter of self-preservation.
- **Their tattoos are not magical and give no Armor Protection**, whatever they believe.
- Once **one-third** are seriously wounded or slain, the rest **Evade** on the next round.

**Attack:** Spear (2H) 14, **6D6** · **Movement** 17 · **AP** 4 · **Glory** 25 · **Armor:** targe shield

## Saxon Warriors — Morale Loss 1D6+2 · Minimum 8 · KV 1/4

A band of seasoned, armored **heorthgeneat** — the personal household guard of a Saxon lord.

- **Two Saxons per Player-knight.** One roll sets the collective armament of the **Defending** Saxons.
- ==**The shield wall.**== Each pair is one **Defender** (spear, axe, or sword **plus shield**) and one partner with a **two-handed axe and no shield**. They form a shield wall to **Defend against a Mounted Charge in the first Combat Round** — ==on that first round **only the Defending Saxons roll**.== After the first round, **both Saxons attack normally**.
- ==**They do not accept surrender or ransom.** They fight until **three-quarters** of their number are down and out of the fight.==

**Attacks (all Skill 14):** Battle Axe (Hafted) **5D6**\* · Spear **5D6**\*\* · Sword **5D6** · Great Axe (2H Hafted) **7D6**\* \* −1D6 Shield Protection · \*\* negates the height penalty for fighting mounted opponents

**Movement** 18 · **AP** 5+6 (or 5 with the Great Axe) · **Glory** 25 · **Armor:** haubergeon, open helm + round shield; **no shield if armed with a Great Axe**

> **Note:** the Saxon **spear** is listed as negating the mounted height penalty even though the Defender also carries a shield. The general rule in the Core Rulebook (ch. 7) grants that negation only to a **two-handed** spear or halberd, which cannot be used with a shield. Treat this as a foe-specific exception as printed.
