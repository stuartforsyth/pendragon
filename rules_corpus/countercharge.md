**A Player-knight does not choose to be charged. In a Battle the enemy comes at you two ways — the *Countercharge*, when lance-armed foes ride into your charge, and the *Fumble* row of Table 6.3, which turns the charge around onto you. What you *do* control is your response, declared at the Combat Round level: `Set Spear` is the dedicated counter, and a countercharge of your own is the alternative.**

There is ==no rule for deliberately electing to receive a charge== — no brace or hold Posture that invites the enemy onto your spears at the Battle-Turn level. Being charged is handed to you by the dice; the choice happens afterwards, in Step One of the Combat Round.

## 1 · Chance of Countercharge

Opponents armed with lances — **mounted knights, mounted sergeants, Northern prickers** — **always countercharge** the Player-knights if encountered on the **first Battle Turn**.

On **subsequent turns**, roll **1D6**:

- **1–2:** Yes — they still have lances and the room to countercharge.
- **3–6:** No — their lances are shattered, or they saw the danger too late.

*GM Book, ch. 6 — Battle, p. 136.*

## 2 · Table 6.3 — Mounted Charge Opportunity

At **Step 1** of each Battle Turn the commander rolls `Battle` for a Mounted Charge Opportunity — **except on turn 1**.

| Battle roll | Engaged last round | Unengaged last round |
|-------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Critical | May charge | May charge (**+5 Charge**) |
| Success | May not charge | May charge |
| Failure | May not charge | May not charge |
| Fumble | **Enemy knights charge you!** | **Enemy knights charge you!** |

> [!important] Turn 1 is a mutual charge by default
> **First Charge:** unless the Army Commander failed, **turn 1 is always a mounted lance charge** — all pick Mounted Charge and only **Reckless or Valorous** Posture. Combined with the automatic countercharge above, turn 1 against feudal cavalry is a charge met by a charge, every time.

*GM Book, ch. 6 — Battle, pp. 125–131.*

## 3 · Which foes countercharge

Numbers are **per Player-knight**. Countercharging foes attack with the **Spear (Charge)** line of their statblock, not their melee spear or sword.

| Foe | Countercharges? | Charge attack |
|---|---|---|
| **Mounted Knights / Elite Mounted Knights** | **Yes** — except on a charge they prefer **swords**, unless facing long weapons | Spear (Charge) 13, **6D6** |
| **Mounted Sergeants** | **Yes** — trained in mounted tactics | Spear (Charge) 13, **5D6** |
| **Hobilars** | **No** — they automatically attempt **Evade** as the knights close | Spear 14, **3D6** |

*GM Book, ch. 6 — Battle (Feudal Foes), pp. 140–141.*

## 4 · Receiving the charge

- **It is an opposed contest, not a free hit.** A Mounted Charge resolves as **Charge vs. the opponent's Action** — whatever you declared. The charger gets **+5** if using a **couched** spear or lance against anyone *not* using a Long-reach weapon.
- **`Set Spear` is the dedicated counter.** The butt of the spear is set against or into the ground, angled at the rider or the mount. **Distance: Long. Contest: `Spear` vs. opponent's Action.** A **two-handed grip adds +2D6 damage**; a one-handed grip keeps a shield. ==It only works against an opponent using the `Charge` Skill== — against anything else it counts as a simple Attack.
- **Timing is settled by DEX.** When one character sets a spear and the other makes a Mounted Charge, resolve the conflicting declarations with **opposed DEX rolls**. Declarations are made from **lowest DEX to highest**, and the winner resolves first in Step Two.
- **Countercharging back needs room.** A charge requires at least **six yards** per charging character, so two characters charging **each other must start at least twelve yards apart**. Maximum charge distance is the horse's Movement Rate at a gallop in one Combat Round.
- **The charger still overshoots.** A Mounted Charge **must continue in a Pass-by** whether it hit or missed. On a Win or mutual miss the rider sets the distance; on a **Tie (double success), Partial Success, or Loss** the maximum is **halved and the rider must go the full distance** — so a foe who charges you ends the round past you, and a well-met countercharge leaves them badly placed.

*Core rules, ch. 7 — Combat, pp. 132–133 (declaration), 138 (distance), 140–141 (charge), 142–143 (Set Spear), 148–151 (Pass-by).*

## 5 · If your own charge goes wrong

A countercharge is resolved with the same results as any Mounted Charge, so the **Fumble** row cuts both ways — against a couched spear or lance it breaks the weapon **and** triggers a `Horsemanship` roll on **Table 7.2 (Mounted Lance Charge Fumble)**, up to **3D6** damage that **armor does not protect against**. See [[action-mounted-charge]] for the full table.
