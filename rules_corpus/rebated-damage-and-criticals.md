**Yes, rebated damage rules absolutely apply to a critical hit**, and the Pendragon rules outline exactly how the two mechanics interact:

1. **Add the Critical Bonus First:** When you land a critical hit, you add the standard **+4D6 damage bonus** to your weapon's base damage roll.
2. **Determine Knockdown Using Full Damage:** Before halving anything, you use the **full, unhalved rolled damage total** to determine if your opponent is knocked down. This makes rebated weapons excellent for unseating opponents without killing them.
3. **Halve the Total for Actual Damage:** Once the knockdown check is resolved, you **halve the entire rolled damage total** (the base damage plus the +4D6 critical bonus) to calculate the actual damage points inflicted on the target.

### **Example in Play**

If a knight with a base Weapon Damage of 4D6 lands a critical hit with a rebated arming sword:

- They roll **8D6** (4D6 base + 4D6 critical bonus).
- If the dice total **30 points of damage**:
    - The opponent must check for **Knockdown against the full 30 points** (compared against their SIZ).
    - The actual damage applied to the opponent is halved, meaning they take **15 points of damage** before subtracting their armor and shield protection.
