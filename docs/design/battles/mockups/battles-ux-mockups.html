  • Answering completes a step (new). No Next buttons: each answer collapses the step to its one-line result and opens the next. Done steps reopen with cascade undo.
  • ① is implicit (new). The turn opens on Set Encounter; "Retire to the Rear instead" is a secondary button. Setting an Encounter means taking the field.
  • Charge and Intensity share a row (new). Two quick GM records made together; a skipped half greys out.
  • Card-first picks (new). Tap a card, then the knight; crit toggles on the token. Opportunities stay locked until a crit, spent ones are greyed. The GM sees Minimum, Loss and KV here only.
  • The turn script sits beside the step that needs it, and the Force token glows at Set Encounter, Negate at Morale. Bedegraine's "always choose the Knifemen" would make the GM set Knifemen at ②; shown here as a reminder.
  • Keyboard: C/S/F/X outcome chips, R/V/P/K postures, Enter for the step's main action.

  • Lanes by knight: each knight's fight reads as one unit. The lane header carries everything about that knight, including the Multiple Opponents warning and posture rounds ("withdraws after this round").
  • One stat header per foe type replaces nine repeated stat lines; attacks in it are still clickable to roll.

  • Morale is the biggest thing on screen: the number, a draining fill that turns bloodied, and a word (Steady, Shaken, Bloodied, Breaking), so it never relies on colour alone. The last change and its reason sit under it.
  • The centre tells the turn's story: a result banner, then the GM's card beside the knights' picks with their shield tokens. Players see names and flavour only, never Minimum, Loss or KV.
  • Today's battlefield lists every card name; cards in play are outlined, spent Opportunities struck through.
  • Each knight finds themselves in the bottom row: shield, Glory, effect chips (red harm, green boon, grey state) and the last thing that happened to them.
