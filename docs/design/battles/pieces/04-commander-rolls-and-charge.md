# Piece 4 — Commander rolls & the first charge — CLOSED 2026-10-17

## Decisions so far (accepted 2026-10-17)
1. Army Commander rolls (Clash+): outcome-only entry per side in Mustering; optional "roll for me" for NPC commanders (Battle, default 2D6+10, Table 6.2 ticklist); folded Table 6.2 reference; saved battles can pre-set outcomes; Fight/Skirmish skip.
2. Effects become tokens: friendly success → day's First charge on; friendly fumble → first charge off + "−5 Battle this turn" reminder on the board for turn 1; enemy success → "Force Encounter" token (once; skips Intensity + picks); enemy fumble → "Negate Morale loss" token (once, at step ⑥). Tokens shown on the Morale strip until spent.
3. Multi-day: commander rolls again each day (extension of the "each day is a separate engagement" scenario rule — labelled), scripted days pre-fillable.
5. First charge: step ② skipped; fight opens with Mounted Charge; posture limited to Reckless/Valorous (piece 5).

4. Charge Opportunity (step ②) reads each knight's Mounted pill: commander's Battle outcome + "engaged last round" (pre-filled: fought → engaged; rested / first turn of a day → unengaged; editable) → Table 6.3. May charge (+5) applies only to mounted knights (board shows per knight); may not → no charge; "Enemy knights charge you!" applies to all — pick limited to mounted-knight cards, Intensity + picks skipped, on-foot knights get a prompt (enemy +5 Height; Set Spear possible with a spear — core.combat.action-set-spear); no mounted knights in the deck → treated as a simple failure (RAW). All knights on foot: roll still made (enemy charge still matters), "may charge" shown as "no horses, no charge".
6. Per-day switches "Conroi starts on foot" (sets every pill to On foot at day start) and "No charge this day" (skips step ② all day); pre-settable in saved battles (Bedegraine day 2) or flipped live; individual pills still editable.
7. First charge with a mixed conroi — **GM ruling** (RAW silent): mounted knights must charge, on-foot knights fight normally, the Reckless/Valorous posture limit applies to the whole conroi.
