# Piece 7 — Aftermath — CLOSED 2026-10-17

## Decisions
1. One aftermath at battle end (after the final day's last turn, or when the conroi Leaves the Field). Participation Glory counted per day fought (Bedegraine: 60/day). Every number editable for scenario special cases.
2. Outcome: computed from KV for Fight/Skirmish/Clash; GM chooses for Small+ (RAW "decided by the campaign narrative") with the computed outcome as a suggestion; scripted battles can pre-set it.
3. KV inputs: enemy losses pre-filled from the battle's fights (dead or captured foes — RAW "killed or captured"; fled and unconscious-left-on-field not counted), editable; friendly losses entered by the GM (PKs killed/captured = 1 each, plus GM-knight losses with a Table 6.5 helper); outnumbering none / we 2:1 / they 2:1 (×2 / ×½).
4. Participation Glory per knight = Table 6.6 base by Size × days fought × (outcome multiplier + leader multiplier); leader multiplier for the commander knight in battles larger than a Skirmish, role dropdown conroi ×2 / bataille ×3 / army ×5 (default conroi). Added to the knight's battle Glory tally on top of Encounter Glory.
5. Spoils: plunder only on (Decisive) Victory — opponent type asked here (Table 6.9; the deferred piece 1 "foe kind"), Decisive +£2D3 (roll for me), outnumbering and commander +⅓ applied, shown as £ per knight. Ransom: each captive's existing "Roll ransom", £ shown against the captor. Money not stored (paper).
6. Finale: "Post to Table" shows an outcome banner and per-knight Encounter + participation = battle total Glory, plus plunder and ransoms.

## UI
- Aftermath panel in the Battle tool: Outcome (KV pre-fill, friendly losses, outnumbering, computed/chosen result) / Glory (per knight breakdown, role dropdown) / Spoils (opponent type, plunder per knight, captives with Roll ransom) — all editable — then "Post to Table".

## House rules / rulings
- Zero friendly KV losses: enemy losses > 0 → Decisive Victory; both zero → Indecisive (RAW divides by zero) — labelled ruling.

## Rejected / out of scope
- Tournament (rebated) participation column; storing money or lifetime Glory; bataille/army-level play beyond the multiplier.
