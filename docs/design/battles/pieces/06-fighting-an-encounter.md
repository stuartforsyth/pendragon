# Piece 6 — Fighting an Encounter (in progress)

## Decisions so far
1. (H3′) Height ±5 automatic from the engaged pair's Mounted pills (taller side +5 / other −5; knights get a reminder since they're on paper); overridable per foe (e.g. two-handed spear/halberd negates). Each foe has an "Attacking the horse" toggle that cancels the foe's −5 while the mounted knight keeps +5 (RAW core.combat.action-attack); Pictish Knifemen card flag defaults it on; it clears when the knight is on foot. Large-shield −2 mounted = reminder chip only (some cards already include it, e.g. Malahaut).
2. (H4′) Track only Mounted / On foot per knight. The GM flips the pill when a horse is lost (killed or bolted — no reason recorded) and back when the knight gets a new one. No remount rules, no automatic Alone on the Field, no house rule. User's working assumption: a new horse comes from retiring to the rear (RAW also allows a Squire remount on the field — either way the GM just flips the pill).
3. (H1) Step ⑨ launches an ordinary combat linked to battle + turn; foes scaled to knights with the conroi; doubled on an Intensity crit; Mounted pills + postures carried; ending the fight returns to the stepper at End turn. Glory awards per piece 1 (G1).
4. (H2) Knight rows show "Round n of N · Posture"; flagged "withdraws" when done; never blocking; fresh foe via engage; "+1 round" tap for the Energetic+Valorous extra round.
5. (H5) Per-knight position chip set by the GM: with the conroi / at the rear / Alone on the Field / left the field. Only knights with the conroi count for the next Encounter and postures. Alone on the Field = chip + RAW reminder; a separate small fight can be launched for that knight, linked to the battle.
6. (H6) Captives: "Captured by [knight]" (defaults to engaged knight) on surrendered/unconscious foes; captives listed for aftermath ransom and enemy KV losses; escort = GM sets the knight's chip to "at the rear" for next turn; escort rolls are table play.
7. (H7) Missile flag on encounters (Cambrian Archers, Pictish Javeliniers): commander's Battle outcome → barrage rounds (crit 0 / success 1 / fail 2 / fumble 3); per knight "reaches the archers" if posture rounds > barrage, else "endures N rounds, turns back" (RAW incl. worked example). Per-round mount/rider hits = table play.
8. (H8) Countercharge flag on lance foes (knight cards, mounted sergeants, Northern Prickers): first turn of a day → "They countercharge!" automatically; later turns 1D6 (roll for me / entered): 1–2 yes. Result on the board; the charge is table play.
9. (U11) Battles reuse the existing combat tracker with a battle frame (header with battle/day/turn/encounter, Morale strip, "Back to battle", barrage/countercharge banners, per-row chips).

## Tracker redesign (applies to every fight) — accepted 2026-10-17
10. (U12) Lanes by knight: one lane per knight holding the foes engaged with them; a "Not engaged" pool; the knight lane header carries Mounted pill, posture/rounds, position, Glory this fight/battle, Down/Out. Replaces separate Knights/Foes blocks.
11. (U13) One shared stat header per adversary type (characteristics, clickable attacks, armour, card rules behind ⓘ); each foe = one compact line (name, large HP bar with numbers, wound state, battle chips, Hit and ±HP buttons; everything else in ⋯); click a name to expand its own differences.
12. (U14) Defeated strip: unconscious/dead/fled/surrendered foes collapse to one line with fate, Glory credit and captured flag; click to undo or reassign.
13. (U15) Descriptions folded into the type header ⓘ (deduplicated); encounter GM notes as one collapsed line at the top.
14. Retargeting on the fly: the GM can change who a foe is attacking at any moment — drag a foe to another knight's lane (or the pool), or click the foe's target chip and pick a knight; one action, logged as today's combat.engage event, undoable.
