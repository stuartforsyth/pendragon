Combat board

A concept for the combat rework: tokens instead of rows, engagements drawn as glowing red lines, and dice that roll across the players' board. Your party, mid-fight. This is a sketch to agree the direction before designing each piece. Nothing here is clickable except the dice replay.

Engage

Drag any token onto any other: a knight onto a foe, or a foe onto a knight. To unlink, drag a token away, or use ✕ on its line. One gesture is one log entry, so one strike undoes it.

Layout

The app arranges each engagement as a cluster: a lone foe in the middle with knights around it, or a knight in the middle with foes around them. No map, no distances.

The card

Click a token to open its card: weapon in hand, Attack (or Attack all), Hit, ±HP, Disarmed, status, horse, Height, modifier. Everything the rows do today, one token at a time.

Dice

A 3D dice engine, bundled into the app so it works offline, rolls the server's real numbers: never a separate re-roll. With reduced motion on, the result simply appears.
