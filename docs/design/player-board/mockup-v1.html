Whose moment it is

The spotlight names the knight first, with their shield. A player sees in a glance that this one is theirs.

2

What it means

A short consequence line the GM can add to any roll, so the result reads as story, not just a number.

3

This scene

The last few beats of the current scene, each tagged with who it involved. Earlier scenes fold into one line, so the history stays readable.

4

The fellowship

Every knight is always on screen. The knight in the spotlight glows. Glory earned this session sits top right of each card.

5

Effects and "for your sheet"

Red chips are harm, green are boons, grey are states. The dashed list is what to write down, built as play happens and gathered at the end.
